    Plate sort sheets¶

    Generated by scripts/build_printables.py from the committed plate 3MFs — do not hand-edit.

    The paper copy for the bench: one bed per plate with its parts numbered as on the plate plans, and beside it which numbers go into which bin. Lift the parts off the bed bin by bin and tick each box. The back of the bed is at the top.

    Print on US Letter at 100 %, two plates to a page, in print order (the ASA run, then B11). After a plate is re-arranged, reprint its page: the numbers follow the positions on the bed.
