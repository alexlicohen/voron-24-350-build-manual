    Bin labels¶

    Generated by scripts/build_printables.py from slicer/bins.py — do not hand-edit. One label per bin: cut along the page breaks, tape to the box. The bin id is also what the plate diagrams and the batch chapters' Sort into bins steps use, and each QR links to the chapter's step overview. The last page is the bin map.
