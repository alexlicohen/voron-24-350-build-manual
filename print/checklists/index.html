    Printable checklists¶

    Generated by scripts/build_printables.py — do not hand-edit. Print with .print-images off (default) for a text-only sheet.

    One page per checkpoint, assembly chapters first (Ch 00 → Ch 14, including Ch 00a and Ch 06b), then the print batches in print order (B00 → B10 → B12 → B13, then B11). Gate A and Gate B are the two halves of the B00 sheet — the day-1 and kit-day gates for the whole build.

    Checkpoint 00 — Before you start¶

    • Both cartons inventoried against the Rev D+ BOM page; Check1 ticked on every line; shortages written down and reported to Fabreeko.
    • Deck panel calipered on kit day and the matching deck_support_* set picked; B01-P2 printed both, the other set is a spare.
    • Nitehawk board confirmed as a V2: PH2.0 on PROBE/TH0/CT/Endstop, XH on MOTOR, secondary USB port present, fan-adapter header keyed. The stm32g0b1xx USB-serial check is written into the Ch 12 notes.
    • XY endstop cable labels read XES / YES, or the re-pin guide is bookmarked.
    • Ten LDO-supplied printed parts binned and labelled "DO NOT PRINT". The V2 usb_adapter_mount_partial_cover.stl found in bin 08-CW2 (B07-P3); the spare V1 cover in spare-alt.
    • Every fastener bag emptied into its labelled compartment in trays A–C, flattened and kept in the kit box; trays stacked.
    • Every "Buy" row in Step 00.8 ordered or consciously skipped. Grease and IPA on the bench.
    • Flat reference verified in five positions; worst feeler gap ≤ 0.1 mm and recorded; working area masked off and protected.
    • Seven practice inserts set (one per coupon pocket): flush to ≤ 0.2 mm proud, square, boss not bulged > 0.2 mm. Height stop set on the press and its working temperature written on tape on it.
    • All 7 rails: end stops taped 15 mm from each end, soaked 10 min in IPA ≥ 90% in their own bags, dried an hour or more, flip-and-packed until grease oozed out of the carriage, rail surfaces wiped clean, labelled X / Y1 / Y2 / Z0–Z3, carriage taped, bagged with the end stops still on.
    • Gate B's bore row passed before the kit, its insert row before Ch 02's first insert (Step B00.7); its rail row is done out of carton 1 on kit day, then both guide sizes fitted rail-plus-extrusion with light finger pressure (Step 00.20). The 625-2RS press row signs off from the same carton.
    • 36 bins labelled from the bin-labels sheet (00-jigs … spare-alt, print/README.md § Bins), each in the bag or box its label names, and the batch → bin map posted on the wall.
    • Measurement log (Step 00.30) holds the flat-reference gap, the deck panel thickness and the inventory result.
    • Fabreeko Discord, Voron Discord and #ldo_motors joined; the gating question posted.

    Checkpoint 00a — Mains safety: read before Chapter 09¶

    • Decided and written down who lands the mains conductors in Ch 10 Sections 1, 2 and 2b, and whether that is a certified person.
    • The "who is in the room" rule is written and posted: adult-only for Ch 10 Steps 10.1–10.23 and 10.82 (Sections 1, 2 and 2b), Ch 12 Step 12.11 through Checkpoint 12, Ch 11 Step 11.67, the exhaust fan's 14.30, and Ch 13 Steps 13.3–13.8 and the 13.9 power-on; on the unplugged sweeps 10.74–10.78 she only records the readings while the adult holds the probes.
    • Multimeter on the bench with a printed CAT rating (CAT III 600 V or better), matching leads, continuity, Ω, DC V and AC V all found.
    • Meter proved live–dead–live once, probes shorted to a beep, both leads continuous, battery good.
    • Printer's outlet is RCD/GFCI protected, trips on its TEST button, and its breaker is identified and labelled.
    • Both of you have walked to the labelled breaker once and know which switch is the printer's.
    • The six PE branches can be named from memory, and the M4×6 bed PE screw was confirmed present at Ch 03 Step 03.6.
    • The SSR "earth the mounting rail" marking is understood: Ch 10 Step 10.82 fits a PE lead to the rail, 10.18 meters it, and the lead, an M5×12 BHCS and an M5 serrated washer are bought before Ch 10.
    • The five VE0508 ferrules located in the kit, for the toolhead heater lead, not the mains; fork lugs on every PSU and SSR lead end; the pull-and-look test at Ch 10 Step 10.8 is understood.
    • Both power-on rituals read end to end; the three eyes-only windows where the machine may be live with the bay open (10.23, 12.11 to Checkpoint 12, 13.3 to 13.8) and the two recovery-only button exceptions, Leviathan SW1/SW2 at 12.15–12.16 and Nitehawk RESET/BOOT0 at 12.19, are known, and the hand-on-the-switch ritual is understood to apply at 12.11, 13.3 and 13.9.
    • The five failure responses are agreed out loud; extinguisher in reach; nobody re-energises a tripped circuit without a cause.

    Checkpoint 01 — Frame¶

    • The counter was re-checked with straightedge and feelers in five positions, and is no worse than the Step 00.10 number
    • 10 A and 4 B extrusions are used; 2 C, 1 D and 1 E are bagged for Ch 05
    • Every extrusion end is smooth and sits flat against the square
    • All 16 blind joints are made and fully tightened, evenly
    • Bottom diagonals match; top diagonals match each other and the bottom pair
    • On all four upright sides the diagonals match and the corners are 90°
    • The frame sits on all four corners without rocking
    • Every vertical's access holes face outward, and the straight 3 mm key goes straight in
    • Both bed extrusions run front to back on their corner brackets, with brass M5 precision spacers under the screws, at 90° to the front
    • Bed extrusions are 65 mm either side of the centreline at both ends, 130 mm apart; all four M5×10 and four M5×16 bracket screws are tight
    • The FRONT tape is on a side the bed extrusions point at
    • Titanium backers are still bagged and labelled for Ch 05
    • The frame log table is filled in
    • 16 blind joint seals are in, each against its horizontal on the inside of the frame; 4 spares are bagged

    Checkpoint 02 — Z drives, Z idlers, Z rails, deck panel¶

    • All four Z rails run their full length with no notch, tight spot or grinding, and every carriage is taped or stoppered.
    • The rails are clean and dry on the outside, with grease inside the carriages only: done in Ch 00 Steps 00.18–00.21 and checked again at Step 02.05.
    • All four rails are centred on their extrusions, each facing the rail on its own side of the machine (front-left and rear-left, front-right and rear-right), with the same 3 mm gap at the bottom and the same nine holes used: 2 to 18 counted from the bottom, no end holes.
    • The deck panel's notch is at the back. The panel sits inside the frame opening under the bed extrusions, held up by eight clip ledges, without rocking. Four M5 T-nuts sit under its four holes, left unscrewed for Ch 09 Step 09.5.
    • _a parts are at Z0 and Z2 and _b at Z1 and Z3, or the reverse if the Step 02.29 dry-fit said so and you wrote it in the build log. Each idler has the same hand as the drive below it.
    • The measured deck panel thickness is written down, and the eight fitted deck supports match it.
    • Every 20T drive pulley sits 33 mm along its shaft, and every 16T motor pulley sits 10.7 mm off the motor face.
    • All 24 M4×4 set screws are tight and threadlocked, and at least one per pulley presses on the shaft's flat.
    • Each drive has four M5 precision spacers, 16 in all: spacers, not shims.
    • Each Z drive shaft turns freely by hand through the belt, with no skipped teeth and no rumble from a bearing.
    • All four tensioner cams are closed, and no drive shifted when its cam closed (the p.46 check).
    • Four rubber feet are on, and the printer stands on all four without rocking on the reference surface.
    • Each Z idler faces the same way as the pulley in the drive below it and is pressed hard into its corner.
    • The four motor cables are labelled Z0, Z1, Z2 and Z3 by the corner each one is in.

    Checkpoint 03 — Build plate¶

    • The protective film is off, the top face is clean, and the magnet is on, stuck down here or fitted at the factory, with no bubbles or wrinkles.
    • All four screw holes are cut clear through the magnet, and a 2.5 mm hex key reaches each screw head.
    • The heater pad is flat and centred, with its pad screws present and untouched: inspected, not re-applied.
    • The thermal fuse is flat against the plate, its screw tight, in line with a heater lead: inspected, not re-fitted.
    • The M4×6 BHCS and serrated washer are on the plate as the PE (earth) point, with nothing wired to it.
    • Four M3 roll-in T-nuts are in the bed extrusions' top slots with four M4 thumb nuts standing over them as spacers, and two M3 T-nuts wait in the right extrusion's inner slot and two M5 in the left's, for Ch 09.
    • Four M3×20 SHCS are fitted, not M3×16: one tight, three loose on purpose.
    • The plate's front edge is 38 mm behind the front edge of the frame at both corners.
    • The gap is equal under all four corners, the plate does not rock, and it clears every vertical and Z rail.
    • The straightedge check was done before the magnet went on, with no gap you could slide paper under, or the magnet came factory-fitted and the check was skipped.
    • The three bed leads lie loose above the deck with slack, none through it, nothing connected, pinched or tied.
    • The flex plate was tried and taken off, and the bed is covered for the rest of the build.

    Checkpoint 04 — A/B drive units and front idlers¶

    • Two drive units and two front idlers are built, each labelled with its corner of the machine.
    • Every F695 pair is flange-out, plain faces touching, with one brass M5 precision spacer above and one below each pair.
    • Each drive's far post holds 4 bearings and 4 spacers, with two spacers meeting in the middle. Each drive's near post and both idlers hold 2 bearings and 2 spacers.
    • All six bearing groups spin freely with the frames closed and screwed together.
    • On both idlers, the M5×40 axle head is seated in the arm's top boss, not cranked, and the M3×40 tensioner is snug in the arm's insert with the arm drawn against the front wall. The arm slid freely in the frame's slots before the M3×40 went in.
    • a_drive_frame_upper has its two heat-set inserts; b_drive_frame_upper has none.
    • Both motor pulleys are the 6 mm-wide 20T (5mm ID 6mm W), not one of the four 9 mm Z-drive pulleys.
    • The A pulley is hub down at 16.5 mm and the B pulley hub up at 6.5 mm, so the two look visibly different.
    • Both pulleys are threadlocked and do not slip under hand pressure.
    • The free end of the belt lies flat on each pulley, its near pair and the matching far-post pair: upper on A, lower on B. No tilt, and it rides up onto no flange. The other far-post pair sits 10 mm away, because it carries the other belt.
    • Both motor cable exits point inboard and face each other when the drives are held in their build positions.
    • Every M5×30 BHCS is snug only; none was tightened hard into the plastic thread.
    • No bearings, spacers or other hardware are left over.

    Checkpoint 05 — Gantry: X and Y axes, XY joints, X carriage, titanium backers¶

    • The E extrusion is joined to both drive units with 8 M5×10 BHCS; the drives are parallel, with no twist
    • Both MGN9 rails are centred and tightened on holes 2, 4, 6 … 18 and 19, both end holes open, with equal overhang at both ends
    • The MGN12 rail is centred and tightened on holes 1, 3, 5, 7, 10, 12, 14, 16, both end holes used, with equal overhang at both ends
    • Every carriage is held by a stopper or tape, and none was dropped
    • Each of the four C-extrusion ends has 2 M5 in the top (backer) slot, and 1 M5 plus 1 M3 in the rail slot beyond the rail end
    • The titanium backers are on the face opposite the rail: the top of both Y extrusions and the rear of the X extrusion. They are flat, every screw is seated, none is chewed by a slipping driver, and the pilot-hole Y backer is on the right with its pilots at the rear
    • Both front idlers and both rear drive joints are flush with the extrusion ends, each with a blue upper Z-belt clip under its two M5×16, the clip notch pointing away from the idler or drive assembly
    • Each XY joint has 3 M5 nuts, 4 M5×40 SHCS, 2 F695 flanges out between 2 precision spacers, and 1 free-spinning 20T idler; the three joint screws are firm and the idler screw snug
    • The raised cable bridge is fitted to the right XY joint with M5×16 BHCS; the left joint is on M5×10
    • The M5×30 BHCS have black M5 washers under their heads, both lightly tightened
    • The XY joints are screwed to the Y carriages, 4 screws on one side and 2 on the other, all six M3×16 fitted, and the XY endstop pod fills the right joint's two other holes with 2 M3×30 SHCS (p.164)
    • The X axis runs the full Y travel with no tight spot
    • The A/B belts are still unopened; nothing is squared and nothing is torqued down
    • The gantry is upside down (motors up, rails up), with the right joint (cable bridge, endstop channel) on the A-drive side; it is turned back at Ch 06 Step 06.11

    Checkpoint 06 — Z axis (mechanical)¶

    • Four z_joint_upper blocks are fitted, each with its M5 nut seated and its cutout facing outward.
    • No 6×3 magnets are used, and no z_joint_upper_hall_effect part is printed or fitted.
    • Four z_joint_lower are on the Z carriages, four M3×20 SHCS each, all four screwed down flat.
    • Four M5×40 SHCS are in, all still light: they are tightened hot in Ch 14, not now and not in Part B.
    • Four Z belts are routed drive, then idler, then clamp, with the teeth gripping every pulley and none twisted.
    • Both belt ends are clamped at every corner: the first under the lower clip and the bearing block, the second under the upper clip's two M5×16. The tails are folded and zip-tied.
    • All four belts pluck to roughly the same note, and nothing rubs anywhere within ±30 mm of Z travel.
    • The six screws that clamp the XY joints to the X extrusion (Steps 05.38–05.39) are fully tightened, with the gantry held back against both A/B drives (p.122). The Z belt-clamp screws and rail screws are untouched.
    • The gantry holds its own height on the belts, and the zip ties and the stoppers in the middle of the rails are removed.
    • The Z chain parts are bagged and labelled for Ch 10.

    Checkpoint 06b — Gantry squaring¶

    • SET_IDLE_TIMEOUT TIMEOUT=99999 was set first (Step 06b.1), and the machine was homed and levelled with QGL (06b.2) before any belt was plucked or any screw touched.
    • Four Z belts measured at 140 Hz over a 150 mm span (Step 06b.3), all four within ±5 Hz. They are set for the last time at Ch 14 Step 14.5 (cold, same session as 14.4).
    • Only stepper_x and stepper_y were disabled; the Z motors held the gantry throughout.
    • The A/B belts were fully slack before any adjustment was made.
    • At every XY joint, only the three joint-to-extrusion screws (Steps 05.38–05.39) were loosened. At the A/B drives and front idlers, both Z belt-clamp pairs were eased only enough to slide, no Z belt end slipped, and all were tightened again at 06b.12.
    • Both A/B joints are checked for rotation against the good and bad reference pictures.
    • At all four Z joints, the M5×40 starts by hand with the joint raised, with no coaxing.
    • The four-point gantry-to-frame gaps are recorded: same-side front and rear within 0.5 mm, left and right within 1 mm (our working limits, not Voron's).
    • Where the square can sit on both extrusions (verify on bench), it shows no light at both front corners: the Y extrusions are square to the frame (Step 06b.11).
    • De-racked (Step 06b.14): pushed back, both XY joints touch their drives at once, and near the front, the gap from the X extrusion to the front frame extrusion is equal at both ends.
    • The A/B belts are re-tensioned to a provisional 110 Hz over a 150 mm span and matched to each other. The final value is Ch 14 Step 14.4.
    • The four Z joint M5×40 screws are still light: they are tightened hot in Ch 14, not here.
    • RESTART was run at Step 06b.16 with a hand under the gantry, the gantry was lowered by hand, and the idle timeout is back to the config's 1800 s; the next page is Ch 13 Step 13.35.

    Checkpoint 07 — A/B belts and tensioning¶

    • Both belts follow p.126 (A) and p.127 (B). Each belt stays in one plane, and neither crosses the other.
    • No half-twist anywhere. At the X carriage the belt teeth face away from the extrusion, and each belt's toothed face stays on the same side all the way round.
    • Teeth are meshed on both toothed motor pulleys (each pulley turns tooth by tooth when the belt is nudged, with no slip) and on the two toothed XY-joint idlers, A at the right joint and B at the left. The smooth back is on every plain stack.
    • No belt rides on a printed part at any of the fourteen contacts in six locations, checked at both ends of X travel and both ends of Y travel (p.142).
    • The same length of belt sticks out of the X carriage on both belts, after moving the gantry away and back (p.141).
    • The two carriage screws and all four M3×8 belt clamps are fully tightened, and nothing slips under a hard pull.
    • Both belts read within a few Hz of each other at ~110 Hz over a 150 mm span, re-checked after moving the gantry. This is provisional: Ch 14 Step 14.4 sets the final value.
    • The gantry moves through its full XY range by hand with even resistance, no ticking and no notchy spots.
    • The probe is taped on the front and sides only and fitted, its lead uncut with its factory connector, its tip 6 mm below the plastic, and its cable in the slot.
    • No hall-effect magnet is fitted (p.145 skipped).
    • Turn the A motor pulley by hand: the carriage moves diagonally. Turn both pulleys the same way: it moves in a straight line. That is CoreXY.

    Checkpoint 08 — bench test before anything is powered¶

    • Heater resistance. Across the two HE0 ferrules, the meter shows a low, steady reading that is not zero. A 60 W 24 V Revo HF cartridge is roughly 9.6 Ω (24²/60). Anything near 0 Ω is a short, and an open reading is a broken lead. (verify against the value E3D prints on your cartridge)
    • Heater isolation. No continuity between either heater lead and the heatsink, the nozzle, the frame or the toolboard GND pad.
    • Thermistor at room temperature. Unplug the thermistor from TH0 and measure across its plug, then plug it back in fully: plugged in, the meter also reads through the board's own circuit on that port (a 2.2 kΩ pull-up, src), so the number is not the thermistor's alone. Expect about 100 kΩ at 25 °C for the Semitec 104NT-4-R025H42G. Warm the HeaterCore with your fingers and watch the resistance fall. An NTC whose reading rises is the wrong part or a bad connection.
    • Probe. Continuity from each PROBE pin to the matching probe wire, and none between 24V and GND.
    • LED chain. No short between 5 V and GND at the chain connector, and continuity along the chain in the order logo, right, left.
    • Fan direction, checked by eye. The hotend fan's moulded arrow points into the heatsink and its wires leave at the top. The 5015 blows down into the ducts. Both impellers spin freely with a flick of a finger and no rub. Ch 13 Verify Fans confirms the powered spin-up.
    • Fan-voltage jumpers. V_HEF and V_PCF both on 24 V, nothing cut, nothing bridged to 5 V.
    • Fan ports. Axial on P2, blower on P4, LEDs on P3. If the wrong fan spins in Ch 13, fix it in the config, not by re-crimping (see Common mistakes).
    • Board-to-board header fully seated, and the Stealthburner front snapped on with no gap along the joint.
    • Mechanical. The extruder shaft turns freely by hand; the gear mesh has a faint play; the tension arm swings; the latch closes; the Bowden clip is on the collet; and filament runs from the CW2.5 collet through the PTFE to the nozzle without catching.
    • Probe height still about 6 mm below the plastic, with the tape intact on the front and sides only. It was set in Ch 07 Steps 07.34–07.37 and checked again at Step 08.55.
    • Cable discipline. Move X to both ends by hand: the parked umbilical never goes tight, nothing rubs a belt and nothing touches the nozzle. The secondary USB port is still reachable. The chain slack and the chain ties are Ch 10 Steps 10.59 and 10.66.
    • The Extruder Motor Ground Wire (Steps 08.43 and 08.53) is fitted as LDO's ESD Hardening section describes or, if it was missing from Electronics 2, reported to Fabreeko and asked about in #ldo_motors. Do not let Ch 10 close the bay with this unresolved.

    Checkpoint 09 — Electronics bay¶

    • Two DIN rails run left-to-right, each fastened with one M5×10 BHCS into each bed extrusion, and all four plastic end caps are fitted.
    • The DC loop is stuck down with VHB at 09.36 as one continuous run, rear-left Z motor to the notch, its middle run 1.5 mm or more off the Leviathan and the PSU. The AC conduit is stuck down against the WAGO blocks, behind the SSR and over the round deck hole. The notch is clear and every lid is off and bagged. LDO layout: five PVC ducts, the opening clear.
    • The Meanwell LRS-200-24 selector is set to 115 V, and the PSU is on the rear rail on its two printed brackets, its front-right corner 2 mm or more clear of the right T's rear fillet and its left end 3 mm or more from the SSR run's open end. No support bracket is fitted.
    • The Omron SSR is on its metal bracket, latched to the rear rail left of the PSU, with LOAD (1/2) and INPUT (3+/4−) identified and reachable.
    • Every Leviathan voltage-selection jumper is removed and bagged.
    • The Leviathan is on the front rail with the Pi 4B mounted on it, heatsink fitted, SD card in, and the 3/4 HAT power adapter seated.
    • The USB adapter is on the front rail with the partial cover, and the DC Ground Wire is attached to its bare mounting point; the frame end is left loose for Ch 10.
    • The IEC inlet module (one part, not two) is in power_inlet_IECGS_1mm, the panel is screwed to the rear extrusion hard against the rear-left Z-motor mount, and three WAGO 221-415 clamps sit in the mains WAGO mount on the extrusion's inner face, just inboard of the panel.
    • The nozzle probe is assembled (collar fully in, PCB on two M2×10, shaft free) and mounted on M3×25 SHCS above the deck on the right bed extrusion's inner slot, pin 2–3 mm behind the plate's straight rear edge; its lead is not yet through the deck. If the 5 mm shaft is out (un-notched, 09.29), it is bagged and labelled probe shaft — in for 10.34's check, then out; refit at 13.8.
    • The XY endstop pod from Ch 05 is tight on the right XY joint, its cable labels read XES / YES or are repinned, and through full X and Y travel it touches nothing.
