    Step 14.4 — Set A/B belt tension to 110 Hz over a 150 mm span¶

    Step 5 of 36 with a helper · Chapter 14 — Calibration and tuning

    These four steps finish Voron's gantry-squaring procedure, split off from Ch 06b because they need a closed chamber. Before you start: Ch 06b cold squaring done (Checkpoint 06b ticked, the Z joint M5×40 screws still at their light 06b.13 tightness, the A/B belts at the provisional tension 06b set) and Ch 11 Part B panels and door on.

    Ch 14 owns the final belt tension: 14.4 sets A/B and 14.5 sets Z, both cold. 14.3 starts the chapter's one closed-chamber soak and reads the heaters on the way up. 14.6 finishes it: it settles QGL, locks the Z joints hot, and logs the two readings the soak also gives, the settled chamber for Step 14.8 and the bed mesh's Range for Step 14.22. Voron's order is kept, tension first (its step 13), then the soak, QGL and the hot tighten (steps 14–17), because tightening the joints hot freezes the gantry's shape, and tensioning afterwards would move what was just frozen.

    A tight belt is like a guitar string: its lowest note, read on a phone spectrum analyser, measures its tension, but only over a known span.

    Gather for this segment — step 14.4 (~20 min)

    Tools and consumables

    • 2.5 mm hex key
    • steel rule
    • phone with a spectrum analyser app

    Already on the bench

    • the two M3×40 A/B front-idler tensioner screws

    Parts:

    • the two M3×40 A/B front-idler tensioner screws · already on the bench
    • 2.5 mm hex key · tool
    • steel rule · tool
    • phone with a spectrum analyser app · tool
    1. Cold, panels on, door open: send SET_IDLE_TIMEOUT TIMEOUT=99999, G28 and G0 Z150 for mid-height, then SET_STEPPER_ENABLE STEPPER=stepper_x ENABLE=0 and the same with STEPPER=stepper_y.
    2. Push the X extrusion forward to a measured 150 mm span, XY joint to front idler centres.
    3. Pluck A, read the lowest peak, not the loudest, and set 110 Hz; then B, back and forth until both match.
    4. Move the X extrusion back a few centimetres and return it, then pluck both again.

    You’re done when Both still read about 110 Hz, within a few Hz of each other, and both are in the tuning log.

    Helper Holds the phone by the span and reads the lowest peak aloud after each pluck.

    Until the next G28, at 14.3, move X and Y only by hand. Klipper still keeps their old position after the motors switch off, so a jog from Mainsail would start from the wrong place.

    The two tensions affect each other. If one belt reads 110 and the other 95, the gantry is not square: go back to Ch 06b, and do not make up for it with tension.

    Caution

    Never send M84 on this machine with the gantry in the air: it releases the Z motors too, and the gantry drops onto the bed. Disable only stepper_x and stepper_y, so the X carriage moves by hand while the four Z motors hold the gantry.

    Why 110 Hz over a 150 mm span: the three sources disagree
    Source A/B target Z target
    Official assembly manual p.125 no number — "cut both belts to the same length" no number
    docs.vorondesign.com — Secondary Printer Tuning 110 Hz over a 150 mm span (≈ 2 lb, "on the lower end of the range") 140 Hz over 150 mm
    voronldo.com belt tension guide 80–100 Hz for a 350 "same as X/Y, 110–130 Hz"

    Use 110 Hz / 150 mm. It is the only sourced official figure, and the only one that names the span, which is what makes a frequency mean anything: the same belt over a 200 mm span reads far lower. The Voron docs also explain why 110: it is roughly 2 lb of tension and deliberately at the low end, so you get a good starting point without stretching the belts. The survey rejects voronldo.com as unaffiliated, uncited and internally inconsistent (survey §4.3, §9 Rejected sources), so ignore its 80–100 Hz figure entirely.

    Good stopping point · ~20 min since the last one

    A and B are both at final tension and equal. The belts are one calibration item: never stop with one of the pair adjusted and the other not. Leave the gantry parked mid-travel with the Z motors holding and the idle timeout still raised; Step 14.6 sets it back to 1800 at the end, once the last hot check has passed.
