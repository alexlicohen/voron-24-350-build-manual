    Step 14.6 — Closed-chamber soak, settle QGL, lock the Z joints hot, re-verify¶

    Step 8 of 36 · Chapter 14 — Calibration and tuning

    Aluminium grows as it warms, so a gantry squared cold in Ch 06b is not square at chamber temperature. A long soak brings the frame to its working size, and then the four Z joint M5×40 screws get their first and only full tighten, hot.

    Parts:

    • the four M5×40 Z joint SHCS · already on the bench
    • 4 mm ball-end hex key · tool
    • 150 mm machinist square · tool
    • IR thermometer · tool
    1. Keep the soak from 14.3 going for at least 1½–2 hours, door shut.
    2. Log chamber_temp at 45 minutes and again when it levels off: that is the reading 14.8 takes.
    3. Then work through the table below while the machine is hot; its row 3 is the hot tighten drawn here.
    Order Hot, with the chamber closed Accept
    1 G0 X175 Y175 to the bed centre, then PROBE_ACCURACY, then QUAD_GANTRY_LEVEL three to five times in a row PROBE_ACCURACY σ < 0.003 mm with no trend; each QGL run converges and its correction shrinks run over run rather than bouncing around
    2 Jog Z back to the height you noted, open the door, and repeat Ch 06b's machinist-square check at both front corners, gantry forward and back No light under the square at either front corner, hot
    3 Fully tighten the four M5×40 Z joint bolts while the machine is still hot, rear pair first, door open as briefly as you can. If a rear joint is out of reach, take that side panel off First and only full torque on these four bolts
    4 Refit any side panel you took off, its M3×12 clip screws back in, door shut. Still hot: G28, one more QUAD_GANTRY_LEVEL, then PROBE_ACCURACY, then BED_MESH_CALIBRATE and open Mainsail's Heightmap. Only then heaters off and SET_IDLE_TIMEOUT TIMEOUT=1800 QGL converging in ≤3 retries within retry_tolerance: 0.0075, PROBE_ACCURACY σ < 0.003 mm with no trend, the mesh Range from the Heightmap panel and whether the map reads 0 at the bed centre in the log, for 14.22, idle timeout back at the config's 1800

    After row 4, take one bench reading of the bed relay (the SSR) while it is still warm. Omron rates it 10 A only on its own heat sink, and LDO's bracket has no published rating (00a.7), so there is no pass mark: you are recording a number. Once the hotend is under 50 °C and the bed under 60 °C, so nothing you handle can burn, shut down as at 13.43, then unplug and prove it dead as at 14.30: cord out of the room, the machine on its side with two people, the bottom panel down and the meter holding 0 V. Within about two minutes of the panel opening, aim an IR thermometer at the SSR's metal bracket, touching nothing, and write the reading in the tuning log (verify on bench). Close the panel and stand the machine up; the next power-on, before 14.7, is 14.32's, with a hand on the rocker.

    You’re done when chamber_temp was level for at least 20 minutes before the QGL runs, and each run's correction got smaller rather than bouncing around.

    Everything you reach past is hot

    the bed at 110 °C, the nozzle at 150 °C, and the frame and printed parts warm from the soak. Keep your arm off the bed and clear of the toolhead, and keep your hands out of the chamber whenever QGL, homing, the probe or the mesh is moving the gantry.

    Caution

    Light under the square when hot, where there was none cold, means the squaring did not survive the heat or the belt change: leave the Z joints light, go back to Ch 06b Step 06b.14, then return. Do not tighten the Z joints cold: it throws away the point of the soak and shows up as an uneven first layer.

    If the QGL runs need all 5 retries where Ch 13 settled in 2, or throw new tolerance errors, first suspect Z joints left too loose at 06b.13: snug them a little more, as Voron's squaring step 15 says. If the retries go on, re-check the six belts at 14.4/14.5 before the full tighten, which freezes whatever shape the belts are holding.

    Good stopping point · ~25 min since the last one

    since the last pause (plus the 1½–2 h soak) — all six belts at final tension and even, the heaters held steady on the way up, the machine soaked closed with its settled chamber_temp logged, QGL settled over 3–5 runs, the four Z joint screws locked hot, PROBE_ACCURACY checked hot again afterwards, the hot mesh Range logged and the idle timeout set back to 1800, then the heaters off, the machine shut down and proved dead for the SSR bracket reading, which is in the tuning log, and stood up again, unplugged. The whole belt-and-shape item is closed. Shutting down lets go of every motor, and the gantry may sink onto the bed: clear the plate before you shut down, and G28 after the next power-on. Steps 14.8 and 14.22 read this soak instead of heating again. The next item is the extruder.
