    Chapter 02 — Z drives, Z idlers, Z rails, deck panel¶

    Time: 4.25–6.25 h hands-on, first build · Sessions: 11 × ~30 min · Steps: 44

    Start the chapter · Resume · Read the whole chapter on one page

    StartBefore you start

    Step 02.01Lay out and label the chapter's printed parts

    Step 02.02Learn the four Z positions before you build anything

    Step 02.03Dial in the iron on the practice coupon before you touch a structural part

    Step 02.04Seat the heat-set inserts in the Z drive parts

    Step 02.05Confirm the four Z rails are already cleaned and greased

    Step 02.06Fit the first Z rail, centred, with the MGN9 guides

    Step 02.07Tighten from the centre outward

    Step 02.08Secure the carriage before the printer is turned over

    Step 02.09Install the remaining three Z rails

    Step 02.10Optional: fit the LDO rail stops at the top of each Z rail

    Step 02.11Turn the printer upside down and pre-load the deck T-nuts

    Step 02.12Caliper the deck panel and choose the support thickness

    Step 02.13Drop the deck panel in, notch to the back

    Step 02.14Fit the deck support clips

    Step 02.15Line the four M5 T-nuts up under the deck holes and stop there

    Step 02.16Fix the printer's orientation in your head

    Step 02.17Fit the 20T pulley to the 5×60 shaft, 33 mm out

    Step 02.18Verify the pulley with the jig, then repeat ×4

    Step 02.19Build the bearing/pulley stack on each shaft

    Step 02.20Threadlock the 80T pulley set screws and check your work

    Step 02.21Fit the 188 mm closed belt loop over the 80T pulley

    Step 02.22Seat the shaft assembly into z_drive_retainer (the shallow half)

    Step 02.23Check the shaft position against the section view

    Step 02.24Close the drive with z_drive_main and six M3×40

    Step 02.25Fit the orange baseplate and its captive M5 nut

    Step 02.26Confirm the belt loop is captured

    Step 02.27Fit the 16T pulley to a Z motor at 10.7 mm

    Step 02.28Bolt the motor to z_motor_mount, watching the cable exit

    Step 02.29Identify Z0 and start there

    Step 02.30Pre-load four M5 T-nuts at the Z0 corner

    Step 02.31Bolt the drive body down with two M5×40

    Step 02.32Slide the motor assembly in at an angle

    Step 02.33Fit the orange belt tensioner, still loose

    Step 02.34Flip the tensioner latch closed

    Step 02.35Tighten the M5 bolts

    Step 02.36Fit the rubber foot

    Step 02.37Check the drive did not shift when the tensioner closed

    Step 02.38Build and fit the other three Z drives

    Step 02.39Assemble a Z idler cage

    Step 02.40Fit the 20T idler on its M5×30 axle

    Step 02.41Pre-load two M5 T-nuts at a top corner

    Step 02.42Mount the idler, matching the drive below it

    Step 02.43Fit the remaining three Z idlers

    Step 02.44Close the chapter
