    Chapter 03 — Build plate¶

    Time: 1.5–2.5 h hands-on, first build · Sessions: 4 × ~30 min · Steps: 20

    Step 03.1Unpack the plate and read the end state

    Step 03.2Work out which side is which

    Step 03.3Strip the protective film

    Step 03.5Verify the thermal fuse (do not fit one)

    Step 03.6Verify the PE screw and identify the three cables

    Step 03.8Clean the top face

    Step 03.9Apply the magnet sheet

    Step 03.10Trim the magnet at the four bolt holes

    Step 03.11Verify the bed extrusion position

    Step 03.12Load the four M3 T-nuts

    Step 03.14Lower the plate on and feed the cables through the deck

    Step 03.15Start the four bolts

    Step 03.16Set the 38 mm front offset and centre the plate

    Step 03.17Tighten one bolt only

    Step 03.18Verify the plate floats level

    Step 03.19Dress the bed harness below deck

    Step 03.20Fit the flex plate, then cover the bed
