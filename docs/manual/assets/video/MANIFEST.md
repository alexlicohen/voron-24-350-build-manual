# Build-video manifest — Steve Builds, *LDO Voron 2.4 Kit Build*

Eleven livestreams, 37 h 15 m, in which **Steve Builds** assembles the
*pre-release feedback* LDO Voron 2.4 R2 kit (December 2021 – January 2022) —
the build LDO shipped him to review before the kit went on sale. It is the
closest thing to a full video walkthrough of this machine, and the frame, Z
drives, A/B drives, gantry, Z belts and panels are unchanged in this kit.

**It is not this kit.** Steve's machine is Afterburner + Clockwork 1 on a BTT
Octopus with a separate Raspberry Pi and a Euclid probe. This build is
Stealthburner + Clockwork 2 + Revo HF on a Nitehawk-SB V2 toolboard, a
Leviathan carrying the Pi, an Omron inductive probe plus the LDO nozzle
probe, and a Clicky-Clack door. Every row where that matters carries a
*Differs* note — read it before you copy what is on screen. Chapters 08–13
diverge the most; 01–07 and 11 are close to identical.

These are livestreams, not edited tutorials: chat questions interrupt
constantly, and Steve works out of the manual's page order more than once.
A timestamp lands you at the start of the work, not at a clean cut.

No stills, no clips, no re-hosting — links and timestamps only (see
`SOURCES.txt`). Regenerate with `python3 scripts/video_map.py`.
