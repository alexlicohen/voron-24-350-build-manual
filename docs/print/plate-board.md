# Plate board

The 22 plates in run order, one tile each: hours, grams, filament, whether it is a day plate or an overnight one, the spool it comes off and what should be left on that spool afterwards. Tap **Printed** when it leaves the bed and **Sorted** once the parts are in their bins. The first plate you have not printed is marked *next*, and the header keeps the running count, the hours still to go and the spool you should have loaded.

Ticks are per device, in this browser, and separate from the step ticks in the Build tab. The numbers are the ones in [print/README](../manual/print/README.md) and `slicer/estimates.csv`; the diagram behind each thumbnail is the same one the batch chapter shows at its Load step. All 22 diagrams on one page: [plate plans](plate-plans.md).
