### G3-01 minor — 06b's split of Voron steps 14–17 contradicts itself, and 06b.16 misdescribes Voron step 17
- 06-z-axis-and-gantry-squaring.md:694: "steps 14–17 (soak, hot QGL, hot Z-joint tighten, restart) are handed to Ch 14 at Step 06b.16".
- 06-z-axis-and-gantry-squaring.md:1020 (06b.16): "Voron step 17, done now rather than in Ch 14 … That step is `G28`, re-QGL, Z=0, bed mesh and the first cube."
- Evidence: Voron `v2_gantry_squaring.md` step 17 is only "Restart your printer with `RESTART`. This will reset the idle timeout we set in step 1." 06b.16 does run RESTART, so step 17 is not handed to Ch 14 (14.6 row 4 resets the timeout with `SET_IDLE_TIMEOUT TIMEOUT=1800` instead). The G28 → cube sequence is Ch 13 Step 13.35 onward, not step 17.
- Fix: line 694 → "steps 14–16 (soak, hot QGL, hot Z-joint tighten) are handed to Ch 14 at Step 06b.16; step 17 (`RESTART`) runs at 06b.16." Line 1020 → "Voron step 17, done now rather than in Ch 14 because the M5×40 stay light for days, not minutes. Ch 13 resumes at 13.35 with `G28`, re-QGL, Z=0, bed mesh and the first cube."

### G3-03 minor — `z_joint_lower` "has no larger bore / largest hole is 4.0 mm" is wrong
- print/B05-z-joints-and-z-chain.md:33–34: "four M3 holes caliper 3.40 mm … — there is no larger bore in this part."
- print/B05-z-joints-and-z-chain.md:119: "There is none: the part's largest hole is 4.0 mm".
- Evidence: STL slice of `z_joint_lower_x4.stl` shows the four Ø3.4 holes (15.0 × 16.0, correct) **and** a tapered central bore Ø7.5 at the domed face narrowing to Ø5.2 over ~21.5 mm. That is the bore the Z-joint M5×40 passes through (Ch 06 Step 06.9 "domed top with the central bore", 06.14 "up through the lower joint's bore"; manual p.115).
- Fix: line 34 → "… on a 15.0 × 16.0 mm pattern; the tapered central bore (≈Ø5.2 at its narrow end) is the M5×40 clearance and needs no caliper gate." Line 119 → "There is none: the central bore tapers from about Ø7.5 to Ø5.2 for the M5×40, and the M3 pattern is what a caliper checks."
