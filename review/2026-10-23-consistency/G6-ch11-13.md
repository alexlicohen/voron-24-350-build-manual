### G6-01 major — Ch 13 tells you to watch the bay live, but Part A has already closed it with an opaque panel, and 00a forbids a live open bay after Ch 12
- 11-skirts-panels-door.md:792 `bottom panel, acrylic, black, 469×469×4 mm` and 11-skirts-panels-door.md:235 "the next plug-in is Ch 13's hand-on-the-switch power-on at 13.3, with the bay closed."
- 00a-mains-safety.md:323 "13.3 repeats the ritual on the closed machine", and 00a-mains-safety.md:334 "the machine is live with the bay open **only** at 10.23 and from 12.11 to Checkpoint 12". The same step says the WAGO blocks, the PSU terminal block and the SSR screws "stay exposed until Ch 11".
- Ch 13 assumes the opposite:
  - 13-initial-startup.md:86 (13.1 Check): "You can see the toolhead, all four Z drives, the electronics bay and the PSU without moving anything."
  - :104 and :113 (13.2): re-read the SSR terminals and the Leviathan jumpers "against the table"; Check: "Every DC duct still open so you can watch the bay". No step opens the bottom panel.
  - :139 (13.3 Check): "PSU green LED, Pi power and activity LEDs". All three sit behind the black panel.
  - :259 (13.8 Do): "Lift the machine onto blocks or lay it on its side so the bay is visible … watch the SSR's indicator LED". The machine is live here.
  - :1718–1719 (What-if rows keyed on the SSR LED being on or off), :1761 (Checkpoint "the SSR LED tracks the bed"), and :1247 (13.43 Helper watches the Pi's activity LED).
- Evidence: the 4 mm black acrylic is opaque, and 11.21 bonds it on before Ch 13. Step 13.8 can only be done one of two ways: skip the check, or swing the panel open on a live, tipped machine with the PSU's L/N screws exposed. 00a.11 rules out the second.
- Fix: this needs Alex's ruling, because it is mains policy. There are two options:
  - (a) Keep the bay closed while live. 13.1 Check: "…the toolhead and all four Z drives…" (drop the bay and the PSU). 13.2: add Do 1b, "Machine on its side, swing the bottom panel down, re-read the table, then close it and stand the machine upright before 13.3." 13.3 Check: "toolboard 3V3 and 24V LEDs lit, KlipperScreen boots on the touchscreen, Mainsail reachable". 13.8 Do: "Set the **Bed** target to 50 and watch the graph." Turn the SSR-LED What-if rows into: power off, unplug, 0 V as at 11.67, open the panel, meter SSR INPUT 3/4 and LOAD 1/2 cold. 13.43 Helper: watch the touchscreen go dark.
  - (b) Add a third sanctioned "live, bay open, hands out" moment to 00a.11 for 13.8 only, with the machine upright on blocks rather than on its side.
  - Recommend (a).

### G6-03 major — 13.39 meshes "still hot", but 13.36's SAVE_CONFIG restarted Klipper and turned the heaters off
- 13-initial-startup.md:1010 (13.36): `SAVE_CONFIG`. :1043 (Pause): "`SAVE_CONFIG` run and committed".
- :1075 (13.39 Do): "Still hot and still levelled: `G28`, `QUAD_GANTRY_LEVEL`, `G28`, then `BED_MESH_CALIBRATE`." :1087 Check: "±0.10 mm at 100 °C". :1091 Tip: "this mesh is a hot mesh at 100 °C". :972 (13.35 Tip): "Stay hot: the remaining steps run in this same session."
- Evidence: SAVE_CONFIG restarts the host, which zeroes the heater targets and resets idle_timeout. The chapter knows this: 13.30 (:829) says "`G28` first, because `SAVE_CONFIG` restarted the printer." A builder who follows 13.39 as written meshes a cooling plate, which breaks Checkpoint 13 line :1770 ("bed mesh taken hot").
- Fix, 13.39 Do: "`SAVE_CONFIG` at 13.36 turned the heaters off. `SET_IDLE_TIMEOUT TIMEOUT=7200`, bed 100 °C and hotend 150 °C, soak your 13.32 time, then `G28`, `QUAD_GANTRY_LEVEL`, `G28`, `BED_MESH_CALIBRATE`." 13.35 Tip: "…stay hot through 13.36; its `SAVE_CONFIG` turns the heaters off, and 13.39 re-heats."

### G6-04 major — Nozzle-probe pin gap: 13.24 checks 2–3 mm, but 09.30 sets 1.5 mm

- 09-electronics-bay.md:827 "its pin standing 1.5 mm behind the plate's rear edge". :841 Check: "A 1.5 mm hex key just fits between pin and plate."

- Impact: a builder who followed 09.30 fails 13.24 and then moves the bed (13.24 Tip). Part L's reach numbers (O, edge Y) also depend on this gap.
- Fix: 09.30 Check: "A 2 mm hex key slides between pin and plate; a 3 mm one does not." Edit 09.30's What-you're-looking-at to match. Ch 09 belongs to another group, so the orchestrator should route this one.
