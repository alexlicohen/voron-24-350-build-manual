### G5-1 major — Ch 12 puts the Helper in the room during the adult-only window 12.11 → Checkpoint 12, and 12.11/13.3 say "two people" at the power-on where 00a allows one

- `00a-mains-safety.md:83`: "She is not in the room … while the machine is plugged in with the bay open — … the firmware gate at 12.11 through Checkpoint 12 … Those are adult-only." `:84`: "She is not beside the machine at any power-on — 10.23, 12.11 and 13.3. One adult, standing to the side, hand on the switch — nobody else within reach of the machine." Checkpoint 00a `:377` repeats it.
- `12-software.md:378` (12.12) "**Helper:** Reads each serial ID off the screen…", `:976` (12.27) "**Helper:** Reads each count…", `:1456` (12.37) "**Helper:** Reads each row of the table off Mainsail…". All three are inside 12.11–Checkpoint 12, with the machine live and the bay open (12.11 ⚠ `:346`).
- `12-software.md:315` (12.11 Do 2) "Printer upright, two people, hands out"; `13-initial-startup.md:135` (13.3 Do 1) "Two people: one on the switch, one watching with hands out." vs 00a.2's "One adult … nobody else within reach".
- Guard gap: `hooks/mascot.py:60-61` lists only `12.11` (not 12.12–12.37) under "mains", so the build accepts these Helper lines; CONVENTIONS' safety rule ("Never on a step that involves mains … power-on checks") is the intent.

**Fix:** delete the three Ch 12 Helper lines (12.12, 12.27, 12.37) and add `12.12-12.37` to the `mains` list in `hooks/mascot.py` so the build enforces 00a.2. Reword 12.11 Do 2 to: "Printer upright, one adult, hands out: cord into inlet, then wall." and 13.3 Do 1 to: "One adult on the switch; nobody else within reach." (or, if a second adult is intended, amend 00a.2/00a.11 to say so — today the posted rule and the two steps disagree).

### G5-2 major — The Leviathan flash path (12.15, 12.14 ⚠, 12.16) has the builder press SW1/SW2 on the live board in the open bay, which 12.11 and 00a.11 forbid

- `12-software.md:346` (12.11 ⚠): "The machine now stays powered with the bay open through the flashing steps … to touch anything in the bay, switch off first."
- `00a-mains-safety.md:334`: "**Bay open = hands out.** … live with the bay open only at 10.23 and from 12.11 to Checkpoint 12, hands out of the bay. To look or touch: power off, unplug…"
- `12-software.md:475` (12.15 Do): "Put it into the bootloader by **double-clicking SW1, the RESET button**…" — a Katapult double-click needs the board powered, so this is a hand on the Leviathan inside the bay while the PSU/WAGO/SSR screws are exposed (00a.11: AC lids cover the leads only). Same at `:461` (12.14 ⚠ "Double-click SW1 for `usb-katapult_…`"), `:492`, and `:506` (12.16 SW2+SW1 and "disconnect every other USB device from the Pi", the Pi being in the bay). With the printer upright (12.11 Do 2) the board is also only reachable from underneath.
- The toolboard already uses a button-free path (12.18 `make flash`, "needs no buttons at all"). Katapult's `flashtool.py` can request the bootloader over the running Klipper USB device (`-r`), so the Leviathan's normal path needs no button either `(verify on bench)`.

**Fix:** make 12.15's normal path button-free: `~/katapult-env/bin/python3 ~/katapult/scripts/flashtool.py -r -d /dev/serial/by-id/usb-Klipper_stm32f446xx_<id>-if00` (or `stm32h743xx`), wait for `usb-katapult_…`, then the existing flash line. Keep SW1 double-click and 12.16 DFU as a named recovery exception with explicit conditions ("one adult, one hand, AC lids on, touch only the button; or power off, tip the printer, and repeat the 12.11 ritual"), and say so in 12.11 ⚠ and 00a.11 so the rule and the step agree.

### G5-4 major — 00a.6 says the +24 V rail reads `OL` to earth; Ch 10 says it reads about the +V→−V value (never 0), and 00a.3 quotes Ch 10 text that no longer exists

- `00a-mains-safety.md:204`: "…while the bed heater's L and N, and the **+24 V** side of the rail, read `OL`."
- `10-wiring.md` 10.74 row "PSU **+V** (or any Vin **+**) → **PE** | about your **+V → −V** reading: the ESD bond below puts −V on PE…", 10.77 row "**+24 V** … about your 10.74 **+V → −V** reading … **Never 0 Ω**", Checkpoint 10 "**+24 V → PE** reads about the same as +V → −V". Ch 10 is physically right: with −V bonded to PE (10.58), +V→PE reads through the same loads. A builder primed by 00a to expect `OL` meets a finite reading at 10.77.
- `00a-mains-safety.md:114`: "Ch 10's tool list asks for "at least 2 MΩ", but 10.13 wants … *"well above 10 MΩ"*". Ch 10 Tools now say "a 2 MΩ or 20 MΩ resistance range you can select by hand" and 10.13 now says "`OL` on your meter's highest Ω range".

**Fix:** 00a.6 → "…while the bed heater's L and N read `OL`, and the **+24 V** side reads about the same as +V → −V, never 0 Ω (it reaches PE through the loads and the ESD bond)." 00a.3 → "Ch 10 asks for a hand-selectable 2 MΩ or 20 MΩ range: 10.13 wants `OL` to earth on your highest range and 10.76 reads thermistors up to 162 kΩ…".
