### G2-01 minor — Ch 02 says the deck supports cannot be fitted later, but its own Do (twist-in clip) and Ch 09.4 (swap them) say they can

- `02-z-drives.md:101`: "Retro-fitting the deck means pulling the frame apart (LDO note p.29–30; §5.2 W4)."
- `02-z-drives.md:461`: "Doing it later means the deck comes out and the frame comes partly apart (§5.2 W4)."
- `02-z-drives.md:1125`: "the frame has to come partly apart. LDO puts this at p.29–30 for a reason".
- vs `02-z-drives.md:456`: "Push a clip's tab end-on into the inward-facing slot … then twist it 90°".
- vs `09-electronics-bay.md:198`: "if Ch 02 fitted `deck_support_4mm_x8` and your panel calipers at 3 mm, swap them … now".

Evidence: the LDO FAQ text for p.29–30 is only *"This is a good time to install the deck supports. The LDO deck panel has a
4mm nominal thickness…"*. It says nothing about frame disassembly, so citing it for that claim misattributes it. The STL
`Panel_Mounting/deck_support_3mm_x8.stl` is a 20 × 14.5 × 5.8 mm plate with a T-head tab (neck ≈8.3 mm, head ≈11.8 mm,
5.8 mm thick). That is a twist-lock key, so the clip can go into the bottom rails' inner slot whenever the printer is
inverted and the deck rests on the bed extrusions. Ch 09.3 inverts the printer for exactly that.
The deck *panel* is hard to remove once the electronics sit on it, but the clips are not.

Fix:
- `02-z-drives.md:101`: replace the sentence with "LDO's build note puts the supports here (p.29–30). Fit them now, while the printer is inverted and the slots are clear."
- `02-z-drives.md:461`: replace "Doing it later means the deck comes out and the frame comes partly apart (§5.2 W4)." with "They twist in, so a wrong-thickness set can still be swapped at Ch 09 Step 09.4 with the printer inverted."
- `02-z-drives.md:1125`: replace with "**Skipping the deck supports.** Upright, the panel rests on nothing else. Fit them here, inverted (LDO p.29–30)."

### G2-02 minor — Ch 06 takes four rubber rail stoppers "from the Z rails", but Ch 02 never leaves any there

- `06-z-axis-and-gantry-squaring.md:65`: "Rubber rail stopper — from the Z rails | 4 | taken off the Z rails". Also
  `:291`, `:296` ("screw a rubber stopper into the free rail hole") and `:620` ("Refit the rubber stoppers at the rail bottoms").

  plastic shipping stopper". Ch 00.17 replaced the stoppers with tape bands (`00-before-you-start.md` 00.17: "plastic end
  stoppers work if your rails shipped with them"). Step 02.06's Tip peels those bands.

Evidence: neither the LDO BOM (`ldo-350-bom.yml`) nor any Ch 00/02 step lists rubber stoppers. Manual p.26 says only *"If your
rails were delivered with plastic stoppers you can also temporarily reinstall them"*. The LDO FAQ p.114–116 assumes they
exist. A builder who taped at 02.08, as the step's first option says, reaches Ch 06.10 with no stoppers on the rails, and
06.25 then tells them to refit stoppers "at the rail bottoms" where none were.

Fix:
- Add a Ch 02.05 Parts line `staged: rubber/plastic rail stoppers, if the rails shipped with them — bag in 02-Z0 for Ch 06.10`.
- Make Ch 06:65/:291 read "Rubber rail stopper ×4 — from the rail bags (Ch 00.17), if your rails shipped with them; else long zip ties".
- Make Ch 06:620 read "Refit or re-bag the rubber stoppers".

### G2-06 minor — 02.27 sets the 16T height with a caliper, but p.38 draws the printed `pulley_jig` doing it, and the jig is already in 00-jigs

- `02-z-drives.md:30` (Tools: "Digital caliper (… 10.7 mm pulley dimensions)") and `:737–740` ("10.7 mm on the caliper").
- Evidence: the 250 dpi render of p.38 shows the blue `pulley_jig` against the motor face, with its edge marked **Z** at the
  16T pulley's lower flange (the jig's edges read A, E, Z, B). The jig was printed on B00-P1 (MANIFEST, bin 00-jigs). Ch 04
  uses it and calls calipers the fallback (`04-ab-drives.md:21`). Measuring flange-to-motor-face across a 5 mm shaft with a
  caliper depth rod is the less repeatable method. The source of the omission is survey §4.4 #7 ("`pulley_jig.stl` gauges
  only the A/B motor"), which p.38 contradicts.
- Fix:
  - Add `- tool: printed \`pulley_jig\` (Z edge)` to 02.27 Parts and to the Tools list.
  - Do 2: "Stand the jig's **Z** edge on the motor face and slide the pulley down onto it: 10.7 mm."
  - Check: "…10.7 mm on the jig, caliper as the cross-check…".
  - In `print/README.md:128`, add 02.27 to the 00-jigs "Opens at" list.
