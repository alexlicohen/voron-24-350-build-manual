### G4-01 blocker — Step 08.7 puts the two printhead inserts in the wrong part: they go in `…_front`, not `…_rear_cw2`

- `08-toolhead.md:264` "Step 08.7 — Inserts: rear printhead half"; `:272` Parts "`stealthburner_printhead_revo_voron_rear_cw2` ×1"; `:268` "The rear printhead half is the back of the tool cartridge, where the hotend sits"; hardware table `:61` "… 2 printhead rear".
- Evidence:
  - STL bore probe. `stealthburner_printhead_revo_voron_front.stl` (71.1×59.1×27.4 mm, the large half) has four insert bores. Two are Ø4.6 at (±13, −4), the pair SB p.38 shows at the top. Two are Ø4.7 at (25, −24) and (28, −40), the circled ADXL pair. `…_rear_cw2.stl` (57.7×59.7×26.3, the thin half) has only Ø3.0–3.4 clearance holes and no insert bore at all.
  - CAD. The SB toolhead's `M3 Threaded Insert (3):1/2` sit 25.4 mm apart. The ADXL inserts (3):3/4 are offset 3 × 16 mm, which matches the front STL's spacing. The two `M3x16 SHCS (11)` have their heads at the rear end (y 140.5–144.6) and their tips at y 125.6, so they pass through the rear half's clearance holes into inserts in the front half.
  - SB p.37 confirms which half is which: the thin part with the CW1/CW2 "missing corner" is the rear. SB p.41 shows that thin rear part (blue) going onto the large part, drawn from the rear because "E-RV" reads mirrored.
- Effect: the builder melts two inserts into the rear half's M3 clearance holes and ruins it (15 g reprint). The front half's insert bores stay empty, so the M3×16 at 08.30 have nothing to thread into.
- Fix (08.7):
  - Title: "Step 08.7 — Inserts: front printhead half — and the two you skip".
  - Parts: `stealthburner_printhead_revo_voron_front` ×1.
  - What you're looking at: "The front printhead half is the large half of the tool cartridge; the hotend sits in it and the thin rear half bolts onto it at 08.30."
  - Hardware table `:61`: "2 printhead front".
  - Keep the count (2, total 18).
