## Three things to copy from Prusa (Introduction, p.9–20)

1. **The reward ritual (Step 12, "Reward yourself").** Prusa prescribes a specific number of gummy bears at the end of each chapter. It is an earned, playful rhythm for a kid and an adult, and it costs one line per Checkpoint. This manual has checkpoints and CAD renders but no moment that says "you did it, now celebrate". Revali's `pass` pose at each Checkpoint, with a prescribed treat, would fill the gap.
2. **One linear Introduction with one start path (Steps 1–14).** Prusa's covers:
   - how to navigate;
   - labels;
   - spares;
   - support;
   - "read the text, not just the pictures";
   - preparing the desk;
   - then "Go to chapter 2".
   
   Here onboarding is spread across Home, the Bench-mode admonition, the index intro and Ch 00's two headings, and they disagree on which steps are pre-kit and what comes first (F11, F7, F8).
3. **The labels guide and a 1:1 fastener cheatsheet (Step 6).** Ch 00.12 names the exact failure: M3×8, M3×12 and M3×16 mixed in a tray. Prusa answers it with a print-at-100 % cheatsheet. The print section already has a bin-labels sheet; a 1:1 fastener sheet belongs beside it.
