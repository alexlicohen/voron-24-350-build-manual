## Three Prusa practices most worth adopting here

1. **Haribo time, prescribed** (p.17 "Reward yourself", every chapter's last step). A 157 h run over 3–4 weeks needs earned stops. Examples: a fixed "Done" line at the end of each batch naming the reward; the plate board marking the four milestones (Gate A passed, blue day done at B02-P3, halfway at plate 11, last plate B10-P1).
2. **Labels guide + spare-bag rule** (p.12–13). Each label carries its contents *and* a count that already includes the spares, and the parts sit in small labelled bags. The bins already do most of this. The remaining gaps are the 61-piece 09-bay bin, spares/alternates stated inside each count, and the count check at sorting given to the helper.
3. **"We are here for you" + recovery** (p.14). Add one "When the printer stops" note, linked from every Print step, covering: runout pause, power-loss resume, the "bed not aligned" / re-probe prompt, a failed first layer, and where to ask (Prusa chat for the Core One+, Voron/Fabreeko Discord for parts). The Print tab has nothing for a print that halts mid-plate.
