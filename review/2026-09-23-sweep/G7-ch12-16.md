## Three Prusa practices most worth adopting here

1. **A screen picture on every software/calibration step** (Prusa ch 9 pp. 208–215: every wizard step shows the display state).
   - Ch 12–14 are 76 of 104 steps with "(no image — see text)", which takes the best iPad real estate.
   - Mainsail/KlipperScreen/menuconfig screenshots, or a rendered console-transcript card, would fill it. Several already exist in `assets/remote/`.
2. **An explicit finish ritual** (p. 215: "Wizard complete — Congratulations!" → "Haribo time" → Handbook → Knowledge base → forum).
   - Ch 13's first cube is this manual's single biggest earned moment, and 13.42 ends on `SAVE_CONFIG` and "Keep the cube".
   - Append a closing step (e.g. 13.44) with a mascot, the cube, and "where help lives". Mirror that at 12.37 "Ready".
3. **One short warning that sends you out and says "come back here"** (p. 208, step 1: "read the dedicated article … Do not forget to come back here").
   - Ch 12–14 stack 2–4 ⚠/Tip/Why blocks per step.
   - Link-and-return would cut the caveat wall. It would also have kept Ch 15/16 from carrying stale copies of Ch 13 rules: Ch 13 would own the rule and the other chapters would link to it.
