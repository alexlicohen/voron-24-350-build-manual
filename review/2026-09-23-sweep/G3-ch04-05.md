## Three Prusa practices worth adopting here

1. **A real parts-preparation step before each sub-assembly.** Prusa ch.5 steps 2, 6, 15, 21–22, 27, 32, 35, 41 and 44 each open with "please prepare: X (n×) found in the Y package", with one photo of exactly those parts. The generated Gather block is the right vehicle here, but it has to show correct counts:
   - `;`-separated Parts lines;
   - reused parts not counted twice;
   - the LDO bag or box name where the kit labels it.
2. **One cropped picture per action, with highlights that match the text.** Prusa's step photo shows only the action, with coloured markers keyed to the bullets. Here every stack or pulley step leads with a whole Voron page at ~370 px. Lead instead with a crop of the relevant panel, or the stack SVG, and keep the page as the second image.
3. **An earned finish per chapter.** Prusa has "Haribo time" and "Victory tastes like gummy bears!". Checkpoint 04/05 end with a checklist. Add a one-line reward or treat moment, plus a `pass`-pose mascot scene on the Checkpoint page.
