## Three Prusa practices most worth adopting here

1. **Picture the route, with the path highlighted, on every cable-guiding step.** Prusa ch 7 steps 22 and 44–46 each give the route its own photo with the cable coloured. Ch 10's v3 mains and PE steps show LDO's different route. A cropped `layout-v3-overlay` per lead, with the lead drawn in, would fix 10.8–10.16 at once.
2. **Keep the safety line in the visible step, in red, next to the action.** Prusa p.174 puts "Make sure all connectors are fully inserted and PSU cables properly tightened" right under the wiring-check picture. Here, the room rules, LDO's "triple check" and the 11.52 lid action render inside the collapsed "What you're looking at" block.
3. **Give each chapter an earned end, and the helper a job at it.** Prusa gives every chapter a "Haribo time" and a "Done" step (ch 7 steps 43–44, p.182). Checkpoint #1 is the biggest milestone in the build and passes unmarked. Ch 10 gives the co-builder nothing to do in 80 steps, even where it is safe (tags, meter read-back unplugged, chains).
