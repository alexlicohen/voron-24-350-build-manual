## Three Prusa practices most worth adopting here

1. **Show the exact placement for every step that fixes something to the frame** (Prusa: picture first, highlighted part, stated location). Three of the four findings here that would build it wrong are location errors: bed WAGO mount, nozzle probe, Leviathan brackets. LDO's own photo would have caught each one. Put LDO's `build_plate_mapping` on 09.30 and 09.34.
2. **Assemble in an order where every Check can be done at that moment.** Prusa never checks against a part that isn't fitted yet. 09.6 permanently VHBs a conduit whose 1.5 mm clearance is to a Leviathan and PSU that arrive at 09.16–09.24.
3. **Give the helper real jobs, and mark the earned moment at the end.** Prusa hands the second person holding, counting and reading jobs, and ends each chapter with Haribo. Neither chapter has a single Helper line. Ch 08 is 10 sessions long with no payoff marked at Checkpoint 08.
