## Three Prusa practices most worth adopting here

1. **One coloured "checking the routing" picture per loop** (Prusa p.116, Step 58).
   - Prusa ends belting with a single step that holds both belts in fixed colours, pink and blue, with close-ups of the ends.
   - Ch 07 has the step (07.23) but points at whole manual pages.
   - Ch 06 has no such picture at all, and a render of one corner showing the lower clip *under* the idler or drive frame and the upper clip *on top* would have exposed blocker B1.
   - Add a generated corner render to 06.20 or 06.22, and a coloured A/B top view to 07.23.
2. **Parts preparation lists every part, and says where it already is** ("…you assembled earlier").
   - Prusa never lets a part appear in an action that was not staged.
   - Ch 06 stages the upper Z belt clips in its own table, but the Voron manual fits them in Ch 05 (p.91, p.95) under the idler and drive M5×16s, and neither chapter says so. That gap is the root of B1 and B2.
3. **Coloured bullets matched to coloured arrows** (Prusa p.110).
   - Each Do line maps to a highlight in the picture.
   - Here the figure is a whole Voron page at ~370 px. Crop or highlight the part of the page the step acts on (e.g. the p.130 M3×12 hole, the p.143 probe screws), which would also have exposed M3 and M4.
