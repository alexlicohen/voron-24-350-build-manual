## Three Prusa practices most worth adopting here

1. **A true "parts preparation" block, with exact counts and where each part comes from** ("M3×40 SHCS (24x), found in the M3 bag"). The generated Gather block already occupies that slot. Today it lists 1/4 of the hardware for every segment that builds four units, and it prints garbage rows such as "deck panel ×2" and "build plate ×3". Fixing the Parts lines at the source makes it Prusa-grade.
2. **Show exactly this action, highlighted.** Crop the relevant panel of the manual page as the step's first image (p.49 panel 1 for 02.41, the p.25 bottom-gap inset for 02.06, the p.20 dimensions for 01.19). Keep the full page second. Add a diagram wherever the source has none: the magnet hinge method, the diagonal measurement.
3. **An earned end to every chapter ("Haribo time", "Done").** Each Checkpoint page ends on a checklist and a render. A `pass` mascot scene plus one line naming what the two of you just built (and a real treat) gives the daughter a finish line every ~2–6 h.
