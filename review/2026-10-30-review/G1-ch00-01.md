### G1-01 major 3 — Step 01.23 seals the corner slot ends that Ch 02 needs for its corner T-nuts, one chapter later
- docs/manual/01-frame.md:591–592 "At each joint, press a seal into the horizontal's **inward-facing slot** … Slide it until it stops against the vertical"; :50 "16 joints' inward slot ends".
- vs docs/manual/02-z-drives.md:1036 "Roll two M5 T-nuts into the **inner slot of the top side rail** above a Z drive … **right against the corner**" (02.41, all four corners, :1075) and :1038 Check "right against the corner"; manual p.49 (rendered 200 dpi) shows the first idler T-nut touching the vertical and "SEAT IN CORNER: Ensure idler is pressed firmly into corner". Also 02-z-drives.md:394 "the 16 drive-corner M5 T-nuts … four per bottom corner, two in each extrusion that meets there".
- Evidence: "inward-facing slot" (01.23) and "inner slot" (02.41) are the same slot to a reader. A rubber seal slid hard against the vertical occupies the slot end where p.49 puts the idler T-nut, so at the four top side-rail corners (and plausibly the four drive corners) the builder must pry seals out in Ch 02 or bolt the idler off its corner. The LDO listing renders (scratchpad `ldo/seal/w2.png`, `w3.png`, `d2.png`) show the seal in the slot that faces *into the chamber* on a bottom joint, i.e. the top slot of the bottom rail, not the side slot. Confidence: high on the 02.41 wording collision; moderate on the exact slot in LDO's renders (small images, one view).
- Fix (placement stays Alex's call): move the step after Ch 02 (or into Ch 11 Part A, before the panels) and name the slot by direction. Suggested Do 1: "At each joint whose corner no printed part covers, press a seal into the slot that faces into the chamber (the top slot of a bottom rail, the underside slot of a top rail): one edge in, then press the other edge down, as for a T-nut." Add a ⚠: "Not the inner side slots at the Z corners: the Z idler and Z drive T-nuts sit there against the corner (Steps 02.41, 02.31)."
