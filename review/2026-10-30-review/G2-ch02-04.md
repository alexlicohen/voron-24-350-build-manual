### G2-01 blocker goal 2 — The bearing-stack diagram draws every F695 pair flange-IN, the exact mistake the chapter warns against

**Where it appears:** `assets/diagrams/01-ab-bearing-stacks.svg`, embedded at:
- `04-ab-drives.md:247` (Step 04.7)
- `04-ab-drives.md:557` (Step 04.22)
- `05-gantry.md:654` (Step 05.26)

**What the diagram shows:** each pair is labelled top-down "F695 — flange down" then "F695 — flange up". The upper bearing's flange is drawn at its **bottom** and the lower bearing's at its **top**. The two flanges therefore meet in the middle of every pair, all six stacks.

**Why it is wrong:** the diagram's own subtitle says "the two plain faces touch". Step 04.7 says the same: "bottom to top … flange down, flange up". Its Check reads "spacer, flange, plain, plain, flange, spacer", and the diagram shows spacer, plain, flange, flange, plain, spacer.

**Root cause:** `scripts/draw_diagrams.py:394–397` draws the items top to bottom: `fy = y + BEAR_H - 4 if not flange_up else y`. But `PAIR` (lines 412–415) lists them in bottom-to-top build order.

**Evidence:**
- CAD `meshes.npz` puts the flange (r > 7 mm) outward in every pair. Examples:
  - B far post: lower bearing flange at z 69–70, upper at z 76–77.
  - A front idler: z 79–80 and z 86–87.
- p.68's check view shows the flanges outward.
- The 1600 px render shows the flange-in drawing at a glance.

**Effect:** a builder who sights the stack against the picture, as 04.7 tells them to, builds flange-in pairs.

**Fix:**
1. In `draw_diagrams.py`, set `PAIR = [("s","M5 precision spacer"), ("b","F695 — flange up"), ("b","F695 — flange down"), ("s","M5 precision spacer")]`. This draws the top bearing with its flange up and the bottom bearing with its flange down.
2. Add "(top to bottom)" to the subtitle.
3. Regenerate: `python3 scripts/draw_diagrams.py --only 01`.
4. Check the `MANIFEST` "shows" text at `draw_diagrams.py:1890`, which currently says "spacer, flange down, flange up, spacer". Change it to "bottom to top: spacer, flange down, flange up, spacer".

### G2-02 major goal 1 — B03 tells the builder to caliper "F695 flange seats, 15.00 mm, 4 mm deep" that do not exist

**Where it appears:**
- `print/B03-ab-drive-units-and-front-idlers.md:12`: "these are F695 flange seats"
- `:35–37`: "the F695 flange seats caliper 15.00 mm … stacks must drop into those seats"
- `:79`: "caliper each drive frame half's F695 flange seat, a 15.00 mm pocket 4.00 mm deep"
- `:81–82`: "Kit day: drop a real F695-2RS in, no rocking … Every seat reads 15.00 mm ±0.15"
- `:117–118`: the Checkpoint rows

The same claim appears outside my files:
- `docs/voron-print-plan.md:790`
- `docs/print/checklists.md:380`
- `print/00-slicer-setup.md:304`
- `00-index.md:47`

**Evidence:** the A/B bearings ride free on the M5×30 posts between the two frames (p.74, p.78). A seated outer race could not spin, and the belt runs on the outer race.

I measured the CAD frame meshes radially from each post at the bearing heights:
- **A drive upper frame, near post (z 78–87):** nearest material at r ≈ 8.4–8.5 mm. That is a Ø≈17 mm clearance bore, not 15.00.
- **A drive far post:** r ≥ 10 mm, so Ø≥20 open.
- **Lower frame:** a solid boss filling the unused plane, plus open clearance.

There is no Ø15.00 × 4 mm pocket. The STL probe found no 15 mm closed loop either; the only round features of that size are Ø10.1 recesses.

**Effect:** on B03.4 the builder looks for a feature that isn't there and may reprint good parts. On kit day "no rocking" invites forcing a bearing into a clearance bore.

**Fix:**
- B03:12: "these frames hold the F695 stacks on M5 posts; no bearing seats".
- B03:35–37 and :79–82: "Now: dry-fit each drive unit's two halves; they must close flat with no gap, and each M5×30 hole must pass a bolt by hand. Kit day: the F695/spacer stacks spin freely on their posts with the frames closed."
- Drop the 15.00 mm rows in B03 :117–118, the print plan, checklists, 00-slicer-setup and 00-index.

### G2-05 minor goal 2 — In the Z gear-train diagram, the 33 mm dimension spans nothing, and the 10.7 mm sits on an end-on view

**Where it appears:** `assets/diagrams/04-z-drive-gear-train.svg`, at 02.19, 02.21 and 06.18.

**Problems in the "Shaft stack" panel:**
- "33 mm of shaft past the 20T" is a short **horizontal** bracket, about 6 mm wide, under the bottom 625. The shaft is drawn vertically.
- Per p.32 and the CAD (shaft y −33.2…26.8, 20T starting at y 0), the 33 mm runs from the long shaft end (the 80T end) to the 20T. That is the whole lower half of the stack.
- The "shaft end" label at the top overprints the panel subtitle ("outw[shaft end]0T pulley").

**Problem in the "Inside the Z drive" panel:** it draws the 16T and 80T end-on, as circles. The 10.7 mm axial dimension is then drawn from the motor box to the top of the 16T circle, which is not a "motor face → underside of the pulley" distance.

**Fix:**
- Draw the 33 mm as a vertical dimension beside the shaft, from the bottom shaft end to the 20T's lower face.
- Move "shaft end" below the subtitle.
- Either drop the 10.7 mm from the end-on panel, or add a small side elevation of motor + 16T like the A/B diagram's motor elevations.

The stack order itself matches p.33 and the CAD: 625, 20T, 2 spacers, 625, 2 spacers, 80T, 625.
