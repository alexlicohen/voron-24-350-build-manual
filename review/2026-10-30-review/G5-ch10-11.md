### G5-01 blocker 1 — 11.36 glues the cartridge magnets with the marked face OUT, which makes them repel the plenum

- `11-skirts-panels-door.md:1132` (11.36 Do): "Stick each magnet to the assembled plenum, mark the exposed face, and keep that
  orientation. Then glue them into the cartridge pockets one at a time, **marked face out**."
- Physics: a loose magnet stuck on a plenum magnet touches it with the pole that attracts the plenum's outward pole. The exposed
  (marked) face is the opposite pole. For the cartridge to attract, the cartridge magnet must face the plenum with the face that
  touched it, i.e. the **unmarked** face out and the marked face down into the pocket. "Marked face out" gives four repelling pairs.
  LDO's guide (§ Cartridge Assembly, ldomotion.com) only says "note the orientation by putting a mark … use the marks … so the
  cartridge will attach"; it does not say which face, so the manual's added "out" is the error. Superglued, the fix is digging
  four magnets out (Common mistakes `:2283` already names that cost). The 11.36 Check ("snaps home") catches it only after the glue.
- Confidence: high (the only way "out" could be right is a pocket that opens away from the mating face; the Regular cartridge's
  pockets open on the mating face, which is why the plenum magnets sit "flush").
- **Fix (11.36 Do):** "Stick each magnet to the assembled plenum and mark its exposed face. Glue each into a cartridge pocket
  **marked face down, into the pocket**: the unmarked face, the one that touched the plenum, faces out." Pause `:1169` unchanged.

### G5-02 major 1/3 — 11.76 opens the bay of a live machine to plug the camera in; do it at 11.52 instead, dead

- `11-skirts-panels-door.md:2221` (11.76 Do 1): "Through the bottom panel, plug the tagged camera plug into a free Pi USB port."
  11.75 (`:2198`) has just run `G28`/`QUAD_GANTRY_LEVEL`, so the machine is plugged in; 11.76 has no shut-down/unplug line, no
  0 V proof and no who-is-in-the-room line.
- Contradicts the manual's own rule for opening the bay after Ch 12: 11.52 (`:1538`) "Unplug wall, then inlet. On its side, open
  the bottom panel; meter +V to −V", 11.67 and 00a (adult-only, no live open bay). Opening the bottom door also needs the machine
  on its side or on blocks (11.21 Tip), which by 11.76 also carries the side spool frame, a Drybox and the door.
- Flow: 11.52 already opens the bay dead to fit the DC lids, and the camera cable has been coiled at the Pi since 10.81.
- **Fix:** 11.52 Do 2 → "Camera add-on: plug the tagged camera plug into a free Pi USB port. Snap every DC lid on, close it, peel
  the back-panel films." 11.76 Do 1 → "Check `ls /dev/v4l/by-id/` lists the camera (plugged in at 11.52)." Saves one bay opening
  and a power cycle. (Known G4-04, Pi USB ports, still decides whether a third port is free.)

### G5-05 major 3 — Ch 10 never says which way up the machine is after 10.23, and the section order costs two extra flips

- Orientation lines in Ch 10: 10.1 on its side (`:117`), 10.23 upright (`:674`), Pause `:694` "lay the printer back on its side,
  bay towards you, for Section 3". Nothing after that. Section 4 (top-extrusion COB strips, gantry endstop, nozzle probe),
  Section 6 (chains: "Push the toolhead through its full X travel", "Move the gantry through full Z travel", "the chain hangs in a
  clean vertical loop", 10.62 `:1637`) need the machine upright; Sections 3, 5, 7, 8 are bay work on its side.
- As written: side (S1–2) → upright (10.23) → side (S3) → upright (S4, implied) → side (S5) → upright (S6) → side (S7–8).
- Dependency check: S3 (24 V) needs nothing from S4/S6; S5 only needs the cables S4 places; filling the chains (10.65) before the
  board ends are plugged is also the better order for slack, since 10.41–10.45 currently plug the A/B, XY and probe leads at the
  board before 10.65 lays them loose in the chains.
- **Fix:** keep ids, move source order so that after 10.23 the machine stays **upright** for Section 4 then Section 6
  (10.59–10.66), then lays on its side once for Sections 3, 5, 10.67, 7 and 8. Add one line to the 10.23 Pause: "Leave it upright
  for Sections 4 and 6." and to the 10.66 Pause: "Now lay it on its side, bay towards you, for the rest of the chapter." Saves two
  flips of a ~25 kg frame (~20–30 min with care) and stops plugged leads being dragged when the chains fill.
