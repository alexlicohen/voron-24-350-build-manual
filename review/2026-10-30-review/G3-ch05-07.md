### G3-30-01 blocker goal 2 — Diagram 01 draws every F695 pair flange-IN; its labels read top-down say the same
- `assets/diagrams/01-ab-bearing-stacks.svg`, shown at 05-gantry.md:654 (05.26) and 04-ab-drives.md:247, :557.
- Subtitle: "Every F695 pair goes flange-out: the two plain faces touch". Drawing (rendered at 2800 px): in each pair the
  **upper** bearing is drawn with its flange on its **bottom** face and labelled "F695 — flange down", the **lower**
  bearing with its flange on its **top** face, labelled "F695 — flange up". Both flanges meet in the middle, i.e. flange-in.
  The in-diagram notes contradict the drawing ("From the side: spacer, flange, plain, plain, flange, spacer"; "A flange in
  the middle of a stack means one bearing is reversed").
- Source: p.97 (crop p097-8a5e1a03) shows the upper bearing's flange up and the lower's down (flange-out); 05.26 and 04.7
  text say "bottom to top: spacer, flange down, flange up, spacer" — correct.
- Root cause: `scripts/draw_diagrams.py:394–396` draws `PAIR` (:412–415, written in bottom-to-top build order) from the top
  of the panel down, so the first-threaded (bottom) bearing lands on top. A builder copying the large picture, or reading
  its labels top to bottom, builds all six drive/idler stacks and both XY joints wrong; invisible once the frames close.
- Fix: draw the item list bottom-up (iterate `reversed(items)` with y decreasing, or reverse `PAIR` and swap the two
  labels so the top bearing reads "F695 — flange up", the lower "F695 — flange down"); add "(threaded bottom to top)" to
  the subtitle. Regenerate 01 only (`draw_diagrams.py --only 1`). Confidence: high.

### G3-30-02 major goal 1·3 — One Y backer has the chain pilot holes; Ch 05 never says which side/end it goes, or to tap it, and Ch 10.60 then asks for a T-nut under it
- 05-gantry.md:449 (05.16) "the two matching long pieces are the Y backers"; :501 (05.18 Check) "Their cable-chain pilot
  holes end up on the same side of the machine"; render `cad/05-16-a.png` "two matched long Y backers"; :165 (05.3 Tip)

  fixed end onto it with one M3×6 FHCS".
- Evidence: tanaes DXFs: `Y_backer-350` = 10 × Ø3.4 only; `Y_backer_chain-350` = the same 10 plus 4 × Ø2.0 pilots 20–32 mm
  from one end; `X_backer_chain-350` = 9 + 4 pilots. West3D and Fabreeko product photos show exactly that 3-pack: one long
  backer with pilots, one without, the short X with pilots. README: pilots are "sized to be drilled out with a 2.5 mm bit
  and then M3 tapped". CAD: the Y chain's fixed-end screw (`M3x6 FHCS (3)`, x 319–325, y 285–291, z 85–91) goes down into
  the **top face of the right Y extrusion** (x 312–332, top z 88), ~54 mm from its rear end — under the backer, where the
  pilots sit (backer 390 on a 450 C ⇒ pilots ≈50–62 mm from the extrusion end). The X chain's end screws into the X
  extrusion's **top** slot (CAD `M3x6 FHCS (7)` z 84–90; 10.59), which the rear-face X backer leaves free, so only the Y
  matters. A roll-in T-nut cannot go into a slot the backer covers, so 10.60 cannot be done as written.
- Cost if missed: found at 10.60 with the gantry in the frame: swap/turn the backers (10 FHCS each, slide T-nuts) or
  drill/tap titanium in place over the rail and bed. ≥30 min plus swarf risk.
- Fix (05.16 Do): "Sort the three: the shorter one is X. Of the two long Y backers, one has **four small pilot holes** near
  one end: that is the right-hand (A side) backer. Dry-fit a chain end link on the pilots, then drill the two that line up
  2.5 mm and tap M3, on the bench (verify on bench)." 05.17: fit the pilot backer on the first C extrusion with the pilots
  at the end that will meet the A drive. 05.19 Do: "front end = the end away from the backer's pilot holes". 05.18 Check:
  "The plain backer is on this one; the pilot backer is on the A-idler extrusion, pilots at the rear." Re-caption render
  05-16-a. 10.60 (G5's file): "chain fixed end onto the right Y backer's tapped pilots with M3×6 FHCS; no T-nut" and drop
  one T-nut from 10-wiring.md:67–68. Also B02-accent-parts-orange.md:56 and voron-print-plan.md:40 ("Fabreeko's set ships
  pre-tapped for the cable chain") contradict 05.3 and the README; Fabreeko's page says only "pre drilled and ready for
  install". Mark it `(verify on bench)`. Confidence: high on the parts and CAD; whether Fabreeko taps the pilots is unverified.

### G3-30-04 major goal 2·1 — Diagram 09 and the 06b racking checks mix up racking with gantry yaw, and the amber box puts Z belt clamps on the XY joints
- `assets/diagrams/09-gantry-racking.svg` (06-z…:890 at 06b.11, :962 at 06b.14); 06-z…:912 (06b.11 note), :976 (06b.14
  Check), :1040 (Checkpoint 06b).
- (a) "Racked" panel: caption "front–rear gaps differ on the same side", but the RL/FL and RR/FR dashes are drawn the same
  length (`draw_diagrams.py:1494–1498`: every dash runs from the Y line to the wall). The panel also shifts the whole right
  Y extrusion and the A drive 22 px rearward (`skew`, :1477–1479). Both drives sit on the one E bridge, so they cannot be
  offset.
- (b) The checks that are meant to prove de-racking measure the Y extrusions against the frame: the four-point
  Y-to-upright gaps, and a square with its blade on the **Y** extrusion, "gantry forward and gantry back". That detects
  yaw of the gantry in the frame (the 06b.11 job). It cannot see the X extrusion out of square to the Y rails (racking),
  and the Y extrusions do not move forward or back. Only 06.24's Check ("X extrusion parallel … to the front extrusion
  pushed fully forward") tests racking, and 06b.14 does not repeat it.
- (c) Amber box (:1534–1538): "Where an **X/Y joint** carries a Z belt clamp …". The XY joints carry none. The clamps are
  on the A/B drives and front idlers (06b.10 ⚠, p.111/p.117, CAD `Z Belt Clamp Upper/Lower`).
- Fix: draw the racked panel with both Y extrusions parallel and the X extrusion skewed, and label the gap that differs
  "X extrusion ↔ front frame extrusion, left vs right". Put the yaw (Y-to-upright) case in its own small panel if it is
  kept. Amber box → "Where an A/B drive or front idler carries a Z belt clamp …". 06b.14 Check → "Pushed fully forward:
  equal gap from the X extrusion to the front frame extrusion at both ends; pushed back: both joints touch their drives
  together." Checkpoint 06b :1040 same. Confidence: high on (a) and (c), from the source code and CAD. (b) is geometry; the
  ±0.5/1 mm numbers stay the manual's own.
