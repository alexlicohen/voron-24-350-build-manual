### F1 · BLOCKER · 05-gantry.md:05.26 (also 05.30, 05.31, Hardware table, Checkpoint 05)
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Push an M5×40 SHCS up through the joint's upper plate, then stack onto it, in order: F695 bearing, F695 bearing, spacer."
Problem: p.97 and p.99 both draw, bottom to top on the bolt: **M5 shim, F695 flange DOWN, F695 flange UP, M5 shim** — two shims per joint (four in the chapter), flanges out, the same sandwich as every other F695 pair in the build. The chapter gives one spacer, on one side only, and never states flange orientation — the only F695 stack in Ch 04–05 without an orientation call-out, built at the end of a long session. Flange-in = no belt channel; a missing lower shim drops the pair 1 mm and leaves the inner races unclamped. Both joints must be reopened (3× M5×40 + idler each) once noticed — probably in Ch 07 as belt rub. Hardware table ("M5 precision spacer, brass | 2"), 05.31's check ("one spacer each") and Checkpoint 05 ("1 precision spacer") all confirm the wrong build.
Fix: 05.26 Do: "…then thread onto it, **bottom to top**: (1) M5 precision spacer; (2) F695, **flange down**; (3) F695, **flange up**; (4) M5 precision spacer — the same flange-out sandwich as the front idlers (Step 04.7), two spacers." Check: "Four items on the bolt; from the side you read spacer, flange, plain, plain, flange, spacer — no flange in the middle." Parts: "M5 precision spacer ×2". Mirror at 05.30 (Parts + Do), 05.31 Check "two spacers each", Hardware table 4, Checkpoint "2 precision spacers". Image: add the two XY-joint columns to `assets/diagrams/01-ab-bearing-stacks.svg` (exists) and embed it at 05.26/05.30, or crop p.97's stack (assets/manual-pages/manual-p097.png) as a second image line.

### F2 · BLOCKER · 04-ab-drives.md:04.10 (also 04.5, 04.11, 04.12, 04.17)
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Leave it barely engaged: this is the belt tensioner and it is set with the belt on in Ch 07 (p.124–145). Preloading it now only makes Ch 07 harder."
Problem: The roles of the two bolts are swapped. The M5×40 is the idler **axle**: its head sits in a 9 mm counterbore in the tension arm's top boss (measured on `[a]_tensioner_right.stl`), it passes spacer–bearing–bearing–spacer and threads into the M5 nut in the arm's foot, clamping the stack inside the arm; arm, bolt and bearings slide together in the frame's slots. The M3×40 (p.67) is the **tensioner**: through the frame's side wall into the arm's heat-set insert (a 4.7 mm pocket 4.5 mm deep opening into a 13.5 mm × 3.5 mm clearance bore — the STL confirms 04.4's description). Ch 07 Step 07.5 says exactly this ("each carries an M3×40 whose only job is belt tension") and never touches the M5×40, so a builder following 04.10 leaves both axles loose for the life of the machine: stacks rattle, bearings tilt on the shank, belts track badly — found in Ch 07 with the belts on. The same wrong model appears in 04.5 ("what the vertical M5×40 tension screw pulls against"), 04.11 ("clamps the two frame halves together"), 04.12 ("M3×40 head with its washer in the side of the arm" — it is in the side of the **frame**, p.68 left circle) and 04.17.
Fix: 04.10 Do: "Drop the M5×40 in from the top, through the arm's top boss, and run it down into the M5 nut in the arm's foot until the head seats and the stack is clamped — firm; it threads into a steel nut, not plastic. This bolt is the idler's axle, not the tensioner." Check: "Head seated in the arm's top boss, both bearings spin free, and the whole arm — bolt and bearings with it — still slides fore-aft in the frame's slots when you push it (verify on bench)." 04.11 Do: "…enter it horizontally through the frame's side wall and thread it into the heat-set insert in the arm's back wall. This is the belt tensioner — turning it in pulls the arm toward the wall. Run it in only until the arm is drawn up to the wall, no further; Ch 07 Step 07.5 backs it out to set tension." 04.5 "What": "the nut the M5×40 axle threads into, so the stack is clamped inside the arm and moves with it." 04.4/04.9 "What": describe the arm as a C: top boss (M5 head counterbore), foot (M5 nut pocket), back wall (M3 bore with the insert) — there is no "bearing eye". 04.12: "in the side of the frame". 04.17 mirror of 04.10.

### F3 · MAJOR · 04-ab-drives.md:04.24 (also 04.33)
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Sit the pulley jig flat on the motor's front face and rest the underside of the pulley's toothed section on the jig step marked A."
Problem: `pulley_jig.stl` is a 47 × 32 × 3.2 mm plate; lying flat it can only gauge 3.2 mm. p.75/p.79 show it standing on its long edge on the motor face, letters upright, its half-round notch straddling the motor's raised pilot boss; on the STL outline the B step is 6.5 mm and the A step 16.5 mm above that edge. A first-timer laying it flat finds no 16.5 mm step and improvises with a caliper — or worse, sets both pulleys at the jig thickness. The Check ("compare against p.75's elevation") does not catch a mis-set height.
Fix: 04.24 Do: "Stand the jig **on its long edge** on the motor's face, letters upright, the half-round notch around the raised boss at the shaft. Push the pulley down the shaft until its lower flange rests on the step marked **A** — 16.5 mm above the motor face (p.75). Ignore the other two steps." 04.33: same with **B** (6.5 mm). Image: a bench photo of the jig standing on a motor (none exists; `photo_wanted.py` list).

### F4 · MAJOR · 05-gantry.md:05.41 (also Read-first bullet 6, 05.6, 05.8)
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Turn the U-frame gantry over so the A/B motors point up and both MGN9 carriages face you. Everything from p.85 on was built inverted; this is the orientation the machine actually uses."
Problem: Inverted. In the machine the motors hang **below** the bridge and the Y rails face **down**: p.116 shows the installed gantry with the motor bodies under the rear beam and the cable bridge rising above; the backer README puts the Y backers on top precisely because the rails are underneath; the chapter's own CAD captions (05.15 "rails on the undersides", 05.18 "backers on top") and Ch 04 Step 04.26 ("right way up — motor below the frames") say the same. So p.85–105 is built the way the machine runs, and p.106's flip turns it **upside down** so the X axis can be dropped onto up-facing carriages. Read-first bullet 6, 05.6 ("motor pointing down — the assembly stays upside down") and 05.8 repeat the inversion; 05.17 ("becomes the top of the Y axis in the finished machine") contradicts it two pages later. Ch 06 Step 06.11 has inherited it ("Turn the gantry the right way up — motors up") — followed literally that installs the gantry inverted with the Z bearing blocks on top.
Fix: Read-first: "Through p.105 the gantry is built the way it runs — motors hanging down, Y rails underneath. p.106 turns it upside down (motors up, rails up) so the X axis can be lowered onto the carriages; it is turned back in Ch 06." 05.6: "motor pointing **down** — the machine's own orientation (p.83, p.116)". 05.41 title "Turn the gantry upside down"; Do: "Turn it over so the motors point up and both Y rails with their carriages face you. It stays upside down through Step 05.48 and is turned back at Ch 06 Step 06.11." Fix Ch 06 06.11 to match (out of scope here — flagged below).

### F5 · MAJOR · 05-gantry.md:05.14 (also 05.13, 05.11 ⚠, Checkpoint 05)
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Four ends, each with one M5 and one M3 T-nut in addition to the pair from Step 05.13 — three M5 and one M3 per end."
Problem: Two different slots, never named. The 05.13 pair goes in the face **opposite** the rail (p.89 — the face the idler's and drive frame's top flanges bolt through, and the backer face). The 05.14 M5+M3 go in the **rail's own slot**, in the 25 mm of bare slot beyond each rail end (p.90 — that is why LDO keeps the rail's end holes empty and why 05.10 leaves 25 mm free; the CAD in 05.4 shows nuts on two faces at each C end). 05.14's "the free slot beyond the rail end" is correct but oblique; the Check and Checkpoint total ("three M5 and one M3 per end") reads as one slot, and 05.13 says only "the slot … the highlight shows". A builder who puts all four in the top slot finds out in Ch 06 when the Z bearing block's M5×30 has no nut — idler or drive off, rail-end clearance fiddling, ~30 min per corner.
Fix: 05.13 Do: "…into the slot on the face **opposite the rail** — the face the backer goes on at 05.17. They are for the idler's and the drive frame's top flanges." 05.14 Do: "…into the **rail's own slot**, in the bare 25 mm beyond each rail end: M5 outermost, M3 just inboard (p.90 blow-ups). Not the top slot." Check: "Per end: 2× M5 in the top (backer) slot, 1× M5 + 1× M3 in the rail slot." Checkpoint the same. 05.11 ⚠: "…because the end holes sit over the M5/M3 nuts of Step 05.14."

### F6 · MAJOR · 05-gantry.md:05.42
Persona: first-time
Quote: "The left joint goes to the left Y rail, the right joint (cable bridge, endstop channel) to the right."
Problem: After 05.41's flip the machine's right-hand Y axis is on the builder's left (p.106 draws the bridge joint on the image-left for this reason). "Right" is ambiguous at the one moment it matters; the wrong choice puts the endstop channel and bridge on the B side with the X rail facing the rear, and the X axis comes off again after 05.43's six bolts.
Fix: Do: "Right joint (cable bridge, endstop channel) onto the Y axis that carries the **A drive** — the drive with the cutout and the two inserts. With the gantry upside down that axis is on your **left** as you stand at the idler end; p.106 draws it there. The MGN12 rail then faces the idlers (the front)." Check: add "the MGN12 rail faces the idler end".

### F7 · MINOR · 04-ab-drives.md:04.20 (also 04.29)
Persona: first-time
Quote: "lay `a_drive_frame_upper` on the bench with its heat-set inserts facing down against the bench, so the two bearing posts point up at you. Drop an M5×30 BHCS through each of the two 5.4 mm holes"
Problem: The bolts must enter from the flat (insert) face — the face now against the bench (p.73). With the frame posts-up a builder drops them in from above, heads on the post side, and finds the lower frame will not close after six bearings are loaded. (Hole data checks out: two 5.4 mm holes 37 mm apart; two 4.7 mm insert pockets 56 mm apart.)
Fix: "With the frame flat face **up** (p.73), drop the two M5×30 BHCS into the two 5.4 mm holes either side of the motor bore. Holding the heads, turn the frame over onto the bench so the heads are underneath and the two threads stand up (p.74). The stacks build on these threads."

## Extra lens
- **Ch 04 bearing stacks:** unambiguous — every stack states order and flange orientation in text, the SVG, and the manual drawings agree (p.65/69/74/78 verified flange-out, two shims mid-stack on the far bolt). Only the "post" wording and the left/right anchoring (F8) and the bolt-insertion sequence (F7) are soft.
- **Pulley heights:** numbers, hub direction and the A/B pairing are right and cross-checked (idler lower frames 21.6/11.6 mm on the STL = 16.5 − 6.5). The jig posture is wrong (F3).
- **Ch 05 rail alignment:** jigs, second-hole rule, 25/15 mm overhang, centre-out tightening — all stated; fine.
- **XY joints:** the one stack with no orientation and the wrong shim count (F1); post-flip left/right ambiguity (F6). Left/right tell (endstop channel) is good.
- **Titanium backers:** faces are stated in words and are correct (README: Y on top, X on the rear). Good.
- **"Which face, which slot":** missing at 05.5, 05.13, 05.14, 05.36 (F5, F14, F15). Stated everywhere else.

## Cross-chapter (out of scope, for the orchestrator)
- Ch 06 Step 06.11 "Turn the gantry the right way up — motors up" is the same inversion as F4; p.113 (blocks fitted upside down) → p.114/116 (installed motors-down) says the opposite. Likely BLOCKER in Ch 06.
- Ch 10 Steps 10.31-area ("XY endstop PCB on the **left** XY joint") and 10.60-area ("T-nut into the **left** Y extrusion" for the Y chain) contradict Ch 05 (right joint carries the channel, bridge and pod; CAD 05-35-b puts the Y chain on the right beam).

## Chapter verdicts
- **Ch 04 — buildable by a first-timer as written: with fixes** (F2, F3 mandatory). Weakest: 04.10 (axle called a tensioner, left loose), 04.24 (jig laid flat), 04.11 (M3×40 role).
- **Ch 05 — buildable by a first-timer as written: with fixes** (F1 mandatory; F4/F5/F6 before Ch 06). Weakest: 05.26/05.30 (XY stack), 05.14 (reserved nuts' slot), 05.41–05.42 (orientation and post-flip sides).

## Praise
- The A/B identity work (04.2, the pair renders, the 21.6/11.6 mm caliper tell, "one kit open at a time") is the best mis-build defence in the manual so far — all numbers verified on the STLs.
- Ch 04's stack call-outs — order, flange direction, "read it back from the side", the SVG of all six columns — leave nothing to guess; reuse the pattern for F1.
- Ch 05's "load every T-nut now" framing, the LDO second-hole rule with its reason, the 25/15 mm overhang table and the backer chapter (faces, FHCS sizes, Torx, README quotes) are exactly what a first build needs.
- Pause lines say what state things are left in and what not to do — keep.
- Sources on every step with pinned commits; the video timestamps carry "differs" notes where the pre-release kit diverges.
