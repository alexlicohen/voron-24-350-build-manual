### G1 · BLOCKER · 02-z-drives.md:02.32–02.33 (Do, Check, What-you're-looking-at) — also 02.34 Check
Persona: first-time
Quote: 02.32 "Fit one M5×10 BHCS through the foot hole nearest the drive into its T-nut" / 02.33 "Lay the orange tensioner cam flat on the extrusion at the far end of the motor foot — the end away from the drive — … fit one M5×10 BHCS through its pivot hole into the last T-nut"
Problem: Both details are inverted against p.42–45 and the CAD (see ground truth). The foot's drive-end hole is the cam's pivot; the cam's lobe slides into a pocket under the drive end of the foot and bears on the drive body. Following the text: the first M5×10 occupies the pivot hole, the cam is laid at the motor end where there is no pocket, no drive body to push against and no T-nut position that lines up (the T-nut pair is 25 mm apart, foot-hole to foot-hole), so 02.34's "belt loop now taut" can never be met — a first-timer either forces a slack loop into service or stalls. F1's fix text carried both errors; the fix wave applied it faithfully.
Fix:
- 02.32 Do: "Hold the motor/mount at an angle, hook the hanging loop over the 16T, and slide the mount's foot down onto the extrusion beside the drive (p.42). Fit one M5×10 BHCS down through the foot hole **farthest from the drive body** — the thick end of the foot, under the motor — into its T-nut. Leave the drive-end hole empty: it is the tensioner's pivot. **Leave the screw loose** — the cam has to move the motor." Check add: "drive-end foot hole still empty."
- 02.33 What-you're-looking-at: replace "a cam that pivots on a single M5 bolt … its bolt stays loose until the cam has done its work" with "a cam whose thin round lobe lies in a pocket cut into the underside of the motor foot's drive end, between foot and extrusion; the second M5×10 goes down through the foot and the lobe into the T-nut and is the pivot. The lobe bears on the drive body, so swinging the tab pushes the motor foot away from the drive."
- 02.33 Do: "Slide the cam's lobe, flat print face against the extrusion, into the pocket under the drive end of the motor foot — it is a 4 mm slot open toward the drive; the tab stands up beside the foot's outer edge, pointing toward the drive (open position, p.43). Fit the second M5×10 down through the foot's drive-end hole and the cam's pivot hole into the last T-nut. **Leave it loose.**" Check: "Tab free to swing; motor still free to slide along the extrusion; both M5×10 loose."
- 02.34 Do: "Swing the tab toward the motor, in the direction of the p.44 arrow, until it lies flat along the foot's outer edge. The lobe pushes off the drive body and moves the motor foot ~2–3 mm away from it, tensioning the loop." Check: "Tab lying flat along the foot, belt loop taut …" (keep the rest).
- 02.35 unchanged. Common mistakes: add "**Cam at the wrong end of the foot.** The tensioner lives under the drive end of the motor foot on the drive-end screw; at the motor end it has nothing to push against (p.43)."

### G4 · MINOR · 02-z-drives.md:02.25 Do
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Drop the M5 hexnut into its pocket in the orange baseplate … Fit the baseplate across the drive's foot face"
Problem: The hex pocket is on the **plain** face — the one that goes against the drive — and the counterbores for the three M3×8 are on the other; the nut is only captive once the plate is screwed down (STL + CAD). p.37 draws the nut going in from the face you see, which a first-timer reads as the outer face; he looks for a hex on the counterbored side, finds a round hole, or drops the nut in the right side and loses it on the flip.
Fix: "Turn the baseplate plain-face up (no counterbores) — the hex pocket is on this side. Drop the M5 hexnut in, then straight away flip the plate pocket-down onto the drive's foot face so the nut is trapped between the plate and the drive; the three M3×8 go in from the counterbored face you now see." Check: "…nut trapped behind the plate, plate flat, three heads sunk in their counterbores."
