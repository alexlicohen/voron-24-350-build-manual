### F1 · MAJOR · 04-ab-drives.md:04.24 (also 04.33, Common mistakes "re-checked with the jig")
Quote: "Stand the jig **on its long edge** on the motor's face, letters upright, its half-round notch around the raised boss at the shaft … Push the pulley down the shaft until the underside of its toothed section rests on the step marked **A**"
Problem: Two details of the wave-1 fix are wrong against the STL and p.75/p.79. (1) The "half-round" is a Ø23 mm *pocket* in the plate's face (walls at z 1.0–2.8 mm), not an edge notch, and the only edge slot is 1 mm wide — nothing on the jig fits round a Ø22 boss or a 5 mm shaft. It cannot straddle the boss anyway: the A and B gauge tabs are at the plate's two ends (x 18…23.5 and −23.5…−18 from centre), so the jig stands on the motor face *beside* the pulley, tab pointing at the shaft — exactly how p.75 (jig left of the pulley) and p.79 (jig right of it) draw it. (2) The gauge surfaces are the **undersides** of the two end tabs: A-tab underside 16.6 mm and B-tab underside 6.6 mm above the plate's bottom edge (tab tops 23.4 / 13.4 mm). p.75/p.79 draw the tab underside level with the underside of the toothed section. A pulley whose flange "rests on the step" sits on the tab's *top*: 23.4 mm on A, 13.4 mm on B — 7 mm high on both. Only the Check's caliper line catches it, and 04.25/04.34 send the builder back to the jig, not the caliper.
Fix: 04.24 Do: "Stand the jig on its long bottom edge on the motor's face **beside** the pulley, letters upright, the tab marked **A** (top-right corner) pointing at the shaft — the plate stands next to the pulley, not over the boss. Slide the pulley down until the jig's A tab just passes over the top of the hub: the **underside of the tab** is 16.5 mm above the face, so when it touches the hub top (the underside of the toothed section) the pulley is set. It does not sit *on* the tab." Check: "Caliper it anyway: 16.5 mm face → underside of the teeth. If you read ~23 mm you rested the pulley on top of the tab." 04.33: same with the **B** tab (left edge, 6.5 mm) and the jig on the other side of the pulley. Common mistakes: "…re-checked with the jig's tab underside touching the hub top".

### F2 · MAJOR · 05-gantry.md:05.11 (also 05.33, Prerequisites "Ch 00", 05.15)
Quote: "Tape the carriage to the middle of the rail before you pick it up. Sit the rail on the extrusion, slide a printed MGN9 guide onto each end"
Problem: Ch 00 Step 00.17 wraps a masking-tape band (or zip tie) round every rail **15 mm from each end** and line 503 says they "come off only when the rail is bolted down, in Ch 02 (Z0–Z3) or Ch 05 (X, Y1, Y2)". Ch 05 never mentions the bands. Followed literally, the builder lays a rail on the extrusion with a tape band under each end (the rail is 25 mm inside the extrusion ends, so the bands are under it), the guide jigs will not slide on, and a band screwed under a rail end lifts it by a tape thickness — a bump 05.12's "no visible gap" check will not see. Same at 05.33 for the MGN12.
Fix: 05.11 Do, first sentence: "Re-tape the carriage to the middle of the rail, then peel off the two Ch 00 end bands (Step 00.17) — the rail must lie bare on the extrusion and the guide jigs need the ends clear; the LDO stoppers replace the bands at Step 05.15." Same sentence at 05.33 ("…the carriage stays taped until the stopper goes on"). Prerequisites Ch 00 bullet: "…rails cleaned, greased and still wearing their Ch 00 end bands (they come off at 05.11 / 05.33)". 05.15 What: "The Ch 00 bands came off at 05.11; the stoppers do their job from here on."

### F7 · MINOR · 04-ab-drives.md:04.4 Check (also 04.11 / 04.18 "side wall")
Quote: "Push an M3×40 SHCS through the small end by hand — it should pass freely down the 3.5 mm bore and pick up thread only when it reaches the insert."
Problem: Reads as the installation direction. The tensioner enters from the *insert* end — through the idler frame's front wall (p.67/p.68 draw the head on the face away from the extrusion) into the 4.7 mm pocket — and its tip runs on into the 3.5 mm bore; the bench test is the reverse direction.
Fix: "Bench test from the far (plain, 3.5 mm) end: the bolt passes freely and picks up thread only at the insert. In the idler it goes in the other way, from the frame's front wall into the insert (Step 04.11)." Optionally 04.11/04.18: "the frame's front wall — the face away from the extrusion".

### F8 · MINOR · 05-gantry.md:05.22 (also 05.19, 05.21)
Quote: "the assembly with the A drive takes the right-hand Y axis, the B drive the left"
Problem: The two C extrusions are identical until an idler is fitted, and 05.19/05.21 never say which idler makes which axis; "right-hand Y axis" is only defined by the idler on it. The Y axis carrying the A idler (tall 21.6 mm lower frame, labelled FRONT RIGHT in Ch 04) must be the one that meets the A drive, or the 16.5 mm and 6.5 mm belt planes swap sides at the front and the belts cannot be threaded in Ch 07.
Fix: 05.19 Do: "Start with the **A idler** (labelled front right, the tall 21.6 mm lower frame) — this C extrusion becomes the right Y axis." 05.21: "the B idler makes the left Y axis." 05.22 Do: "…the Y axis carrying the A (front-right) idler goes into the A drive, the B-idler axis into the B drive — read the Ch 04 labels." Check: "A idler and A drive on the same beam."
