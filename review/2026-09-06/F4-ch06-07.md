### F1 · BLOCKER · 06-z-axis-and-gantry-squaring.md:06b.1
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Jog the gantry up until the fixed side of a Z belt is 150 mm from the Z idler centres … Do all four."
Problem: 06b.1 is inserted *before* 06b.2 (`SET_IDLE_TIMEOUT`) and 06b.3 (`G28`). A first-timer spends 15–25 min plucking four corners with a phone; Klipper's default idle timeout (600 s) disables the steppers mid-step, the Z motors let go and the gantry sags/drops onto whatever is under it (bed/PEI, toolhead), and the machine is no longer homed so "jog the gantry" and "move it down and back" stop working. Voron's own step 1 exists precisely to prevent this; Ch 13.34 even tells the reader the procedure "starts by raising the idle timeout".
Fix: Move this step to after 06b.3 and renumber (06b.1 timeout → 06b.2 home+QGL → 06b.3 Z belts → park). Add to its Do: "Only after the idle timeout is raised and the machine is homed — the Z motors must be holding the gantry the whole time." Keep the "added step, not in the official procedure" note.

### F3 · MAJOR · 07-ab-belts.md:07.16 (also intro ¶, 07.10, 07.17, 07.22, 07.23, `03-corexy-belt-path.svg`)
Persona: first-time
Quote: "everywhere it is merely turned, it runs smooth-side-on over a plain bearing stack" / 07.23 Check: "Teeth on both motor pulleys, smooth back on every plain stack".
Problem: Topologically false, and the chapter contradicts Ch 05. Each belt turns 90° at both XY joints in *opposite* senses (A: right turn at the left joint, left turn at the right joint), so one of the two contacts is teeth-on. Ch 05.29 says of the joint's GT2 20T idler "the toothed side of the belt wraps it"; p.138 (B return, left joint) and p.135 draw toothed idlers. Each joint holds one F695 pair (under the upper plate — the upper belt, A) and one 20T toothed idler (lower pocket — the lower belt, B). The belt *leaving* the carriage turns smooth-back-on; the belt *returning* from the front idler turns teeth-on. A first-timer who follows the 07.23 check finds teeth on the idler at 07.16/07.22, concludes they mis-routed, and puts a half-twist in the belt to "fix" it. The SVG legend ("F695 bearing stack — smooth back rides here") draws both XY-joint contacts as F695.
Fix: Intro ¶: "…it runs smooth-side-on over a plain bearing stack — except at the XY joints: each belt leaves the carriage smooth-back-on its joint idler and comes back teeth-on the other joint's idler. That is by design; never twist a belt to change which face touches an idler." 07.10/07.17: "smooth back on the idler". 07.16/07.22: "the returning run wraps this joint's idler with its **teeth** on it (Ch 05.29) — correct, not a mis-route". 07.23 Check: "smooth back on every plain stack *except* the two return turns at the XY joints, which are teeth-on". Fix the SVG legend and add tooth ticks on the return-side joint wraps. (Which plane meets the F695 pair vs the 20T idler is fixed by height and needs no decision — say so.)

### F4 · MAJOR · 07-ab-belts.md:07.9
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Feed one end of each belt into its clamp slot in the left X carriage part — one belt into the upper slot, one into the lower."
Problem: From 07.10 on the chapter routes "the A belt" but never says which belt is A. The two belts are identical; A is *defined* by plane: Ch 04 sets the A pulley hub-down at 16.5 mm and B hub-up at 6.5 mm (p.75/p.79), and the A idler's lower frame is the tall one — so **A is the upper belt**. A first-timer who routes the lower belt as "A" arrives at the rear-right drive with the belt 10 mm below the motor pulley and has to unclamp and start over (or forces it).
Fix: Do: "The belt in the **upper** slot is A (the A motor pulley and A front idler sit 10 mm higher than B's — Ch 04 Step 04.2); the lower slot is B. Mark A on the smooth back now (Tip in 07.8)." Also add to 07.3/07.4 What-you're-looking-at: "A is the upper of the two planes, B the lower."

### F7 · MAJOR · 06-z-axis-and-gantry-squaring.md:06b.1
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Jog the gantry up until the fixed side of a Z belt is 150 mm from the Z idler centres. Pluck that 150 mm span"
Problem: "Fixed side" is Voron's phrase, undefined here. The drive→idler run is ~450 mm at every gantry height, so the only span that can *be* 150 mm is the short run between the Z joint's upper clamp and the idler. Plucking the long run reads ~40 Hz and sends a first-timer cranking the tensioner toward 140 Hz. The 06.23 CAD caption ("pluck each on its fixed run") uses the phrase differently again.
Fix: "Raise the gantry until the top belt clip on the Z bearing block is 150 mm below the Z idler pulley centre (rule from the idler axle down to the clip). Pluck the short vertical run between clip and idler. (Voron's 'fixed side' means this belt end fixed at the joint — verify on bench.) Tensioner head is under the orange slider: 2.5 mm key from below, clockwise as you look up = tighter." Apply the same wording to 06.23's caption and Ch 14.5.

### F8 · MAJOR · 06-z-axis-and-gantry-squaring.md:06.18 (also `04-z-drive-gear-train.svg`)

## Chapter verdicts

- **Ch 06 Part A (06.1–06.26)** — buildable by a first-timer as written: **with fixes**. Weakest: 06.24 (which screws), 06.18 (twist check wrong), 06.12–06.13 (lift under-specified).
- **Ch 06b (06b.1–06b.18)** — buildable by a first-timer as written: **no**. Weakest: 06b.9 (which bolts, gantry on belts), 06b.1 (before idle timeout; span undefined), 06b.16–06b.18 (panels/door don't exist yet; no return pointer).
- **Ch 07** — buildable by a first-timer as written: **with fixes**. Weakest: 07.9 (which slot is A), 07.16/07.23 (teeth-on at the return XY joint called a mis-route), Read-first/tension ownership (F5).

## Praise

- The LDO reorder (p.115–116 before p.114, rubber stoppers as a shelf) is explained once, applied at every affected step, and the Check at 06.10 (four heights within 1 mm) is a real gate.
- 06.17's "loosen, don't remove — the same two screws hold the lower clamp" is exactly the trap a first-timer falls into, caught in the step and again in Common mistakes.
- 07.3/07.4 tracing steps plus the "two runs on one side, one on the other" tell give a belt identity check that survives both belts being on.
- 07.27 makes tail-length-equals-tension explicit and measurable, and 07.33's admonition orders the failure causes correctly.
- Part B transcribes all 17 Voron steps with the original step number on each, adds the four-point gap table, and keeps the "light until hot" rule visible at 06.14, 06b.13 and 06b.18.
