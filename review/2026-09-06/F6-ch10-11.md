### F1 · BLOCKER · 10-wiring.md:10.18 (Checkpoint #1)
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Probe every occupied port of each WAGO against every other occupied port of the same WAGO."
Problem: The manual narrows LDO's "all nodes of the same colour" to WAGO-port-to-WAGO-port. The far ends are never continuity-tested before the first power-on: the PSU's ⏚/N/L screws (landed in 10.9 with only a pull-test), SSR LOAD 2, the bed ring, the frame bolt, the C14 pins. Concrete miss: the PSU's green/yellow landed in the L WAGO puts mains on the PSU chassis; 10.18 still passes (each block is one node), 10.19 still passes (chassis has no PE path), and the fault is first measurable at 10.77 — after 10.23 has energised it. Same for L/N swapped at the PSU.
Fix: Rewrite 10.18 as an end-to-end node list, one row per colour: **PE** — C14 E pin ↔ every PE WAGO port ↔ PSU ⏚ screw ↔ bed ring/M4 screw ↔ frame bolt. **N** — C14 N (rocker ON) ↔ N WAGO ports ↔ PSU N screw ↔ bed N. **L** — C14 L (rocker ON) ↔ L WAGO ports ↔ PSU L screw ↔ SSR LOAD 2. Every pair beeps. Then in 10.19 add the cross-check "PSU ⏚ ↔ PSU L and PSU ⏚ ↔ PSU N: silent". Alternatively put the three PSU rows into 10.9's Check; either way it must precede 10.23.

### F2 · BLOCKER · 10-wiring.md:10.23
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Plug the cord into the inlet, then into the wall, then switch the inlet rocker on. … Meter on DC V, PSU +V → −V | 24 V ± 0.5 V"
Problem: Three ritual gaps in the only live step. (a) Rocker state before plugging is unspecified — 10.18–10.20 had the builder toggling it and may have left it ON, so the PSU energises while a hand is on the C14 with no observed switch-on moment. (b) The 24 V measurement is the manual's addition (LDO's Checkpoint 1 asks only for the LED) and it puts two probes on +V/−V screws that sit on the same block, two screws away from live N and L with no terminal cover (S4 photo). (c) 00a.2 requires "one adult, standing to the side, hand on the switch, nobody else within reach" — you cannot hold two probes and the switch. Ch 10 never restates the who-is-in-the-room rule at Section 1, Section 2 or this step.
Fix: Do: "Rocker **OFF**. Cord into the inlet, then the wall. Stand to the side, hand on the rocker, other hand behind your back. Rocker ON. Look: rocker lamp, PSU green LED. Rocker OFF. Unplug." Drop the live 24 V row (the PSU reads 24 V again at Ch 13's first power-up with the covers off), or make it an explicit optional row with "probes from the DC end only, one hand". Add the one-line box at the head of Section 1, Section 2 and 10.23: "Who is in the room (00a.2): she is out of the room 10.1–10.23; back for 10.24; she reads the meter aloud in Section 8."

### F3 · MAJOR · 10-wiring.md:10.4, 10.6, 10.7 (vs Ch 09 09.10–09.13)
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Melt the heat-set inserts into the printed panel from the flat face. Push the inlet module into the rectangular opening…"
Problem: Ch 09 already heat-sets the panel (09.10), fits the inlet module with orientation stated (09.11 "earth pin at the top"), mounts it to the rear extrusion (09.12), and snaps the three WAGOs into the mount (09.13). Ch 11's parts table and the print plan both say the panel is "fitted in Ch 09". Ch 10's prerequisites list omits it, then 10.4/10.6 redo it with no orientation and "(verify on bench)" everywhere, and 10.7 re-snaps the WAGOs. A builder arriving with the panel on the frame cannot "melt inserts into the panel" and will assume something was skipped. The Sessions count and the 10.4 Pause include phantom work.
Fix: Delete 10.4 and 10.6; make 10.7 "Label the WAGO bus from 09.13 N/L/PE" (labels are the only new work). Add "Ch 09 — inlet panel with module fitted (09.10–09.12), mains WAGO block mounted (09.13)" to the prerequisites. Re-point the Read-first line "stop at step 10.4" to the new first mains step.

### F4 · MAJOR · 10-wiring.md:10.12 (vs Ch 03 Step 03.x and diagram 05)
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Bolt the PE ring terminal to the plate's pre-fitted M4×6 BHCS + serrated washer from Ch 03 — verify that screw is tight; do not remove it and re-fit it."
Problem: Physically impossible as written — a ring terminal cannot go under a screw that is not backed off — and it contradicts Ch 03 ("Leave it in place; back it off later only when you land the PE ring terminal in Ch 10"). Diagram 05 repeats "do not remove it". A first-timer either leaves the ring loose against the washer (no bond) or removes the screw fully.
Fix: "Back the M4×6 out three turns — do not take it out — slide the ring terminal under the serrated washer, retighten until the washer bites." Update diagram 05's caption to match.

### F5 · MAJOR · 10-wiring.md:10.12–10.14
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Land Bed L in one 2-way WAGO and N in the other — one bed lead per terminal, never both in one."
Problem: The step lands only the pad's own leads. The kit's braided extension cables (visible at SSR 1 as "BED L" in S6) are never told to go into the second port of each 2-way WAGO, yet 10.14 says "Run the Bed L lead from the bed WAGO breakout … to SSR LOAD 1" as if it were already there. Builder either pulls the pad's short lead through the deck or stops to guess what the second WAGO port is for.
Fix: 10.12 Do: "Pad lead **Bed L** into one port of the first 2-way WAGO, the kit's **Bed L** extension into its other port. Pad **N** and the kit's **Bed N** extension into the second WAGO. Never L and N in one block." Also swap 10.13 before 10.12 (measure the pad at its bare leads before any WAGO is in the path).

### F6 · MAJOR · 10-wiring.md:10.24 (vs diagram 06)
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Land the TO MB end on the PSU's +V and −V terminals. Land the 24V end on the Leviathan screw terminal…"
Problem: The step text and diagram 06 disagree: the diagram's POWER row reads "24V PSU to MB (TO MB end) → Vin 24V / Board — other end on the PSU". LDO's S6 photo shows the PSU end tagged TO MB / TO TOOLHEAD and the device end tagged 24V / HV, so the text is right and the diagram is wrong — but the builder has both in front of them. No rule is given for LDO's tag convention (10.11 already had to override LDO's own text).
Fix: Correct diagram 06's row to "(24V end) → Vin 24V; TO MB end on the PSU". Add one line before 10.24: "LDO's 24 V cables are tagged with the *destination* at the PSU end (TO MB, TO MB HV, TO TOOLHEAD) and with the *rail name* at the device end (24V, HV, 24V IN). Read the tag, then check polarity red/black at both ends." State whether the ends are physically interchangeable (verify on bench).

### F7 · MAJOR · 10-wiring.md:10.23 → 10.24 (post-power-on "dead" check)
Persona: first-time

Problem: The confirm-dead ritual lives at 10.73, at the chapter's end. 10.24 puts a screwdriver on the PSU +V/−V terminals seconds after the first power-on; the LRS-200's output caps hold charge with no load. The first "done dead" step is the one without the check.

### F8 · MAJOR · 10-wiring.md:10.16, 10.58, 10.77
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Continuity from the C14 earth pin to any frame extrusion, measured at a far corner. Under a few ohms."
Problem: The extrusions are anodised; the flat face reads OL even when the frame is perfectly bonded. A first-timer will conclude the PE lead failed and start scraping anodising around the machine. "Under a few ohms" also needs the Ω range, not the beeper. Same trap in 10.58 (USB-adapter ground to "the printer frame" — no fastener, no bare-metal instruction) and 10.77's frame row.
Fix: In all three: "Probe a screw head, a T-nut, or the bare cut end of an extrusion — never the anodised face. Meter on Ω: expect < 2–3 Ω." In 10.58 name the fastener stack (M3 screw + serrated washer into a T-nut on bare metal, same rule as 10.16).

### F11 · MAJOR · 10-wiring.md:10.50 (vs 11-skirts-panels-door.md:11.6, 11.8)
Persona: first-time

### F12 · MAJOR · 11-skirts-panels-door.md:11.11, 11.15–11.17
Persona: first-time
Quote: "This is a five-layer sandwich occupying one skirt position per side … two fan positions populated in total." / Pause after 11.17: "the bay fan module trapped in the right-hand run".
Problem: Each `side_fan_support` has **two** fan openings (p.225, p.227, p.230, the parts render), the kit has 4 grills, 2 retainers, 2 fans, and LDO's bay photos (S1/S4/S6) put **both** fans in the right-hand support (PSU side, seen from the front) with grills only on the left. The chapter builds "one fan module" per side (11.15, 11.16, 11.17 "the second side_fan_support and its fan/grill/retainer set"), then the Pause says the fan module is in the right-hand run only. A builder ends up with one fan and one open hole per side, and no retainer for the blank grills. 11.11's screw path ("through grill and fan, into the retainer's inserts") is also wrong — an M3×8 cannot pass a 20 mm fan; the retainer frames the fan behind the support wall (p.213/p.228).
Fix: 11.11: "Both fans go in ONE support — the right-hand side (seen from the front), where LDO's finished-bay photos show them beside the PSU. Grill → support wall → retainer (inserts) with the fan captured inside the retainer's frame, M3×8 ×4 per fan. The left support gets the two remaining grills as blanks (attachment: verify on bench — no retainer)." 11.15–11.17: name which side gets the fan support and which gets the blank support. Airflow: state a direction (both blowing into the bay is the common choice, so the grill filters intake air) rather than "match p.225", which shows none. p.213 shows the arrow on the fan **frame**, not the hub.

### F14 · MAJOR · 11-skirts-panels-door.md:11.21 (vs 10-wiring.md:10.71 and Ch 13)

### F15 · MINOR · 10-wiring.md:10.3 (and diagram 07)
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Any jumper still fitted at 5 V while a 24 V load is plugged in shorts 5 V to 24 V and destroys the board."
Problem: Not what the Leviathan's 3-pin selectors do: a jumper at 5 V under-drives a 24 V fan; a jumper at 24 V destroys a 5 V *device*. A 2-pin jumper cannot bridge the two outer pins. The safe end state (all bare) is right; the stated failure mode is wrong and is repeated in diagram 07.
Fix: "A jumper on 24 V with a 5 V device destroys the device; nothing here can bridge 5 V to 24 V. They start bare so 10.28 fits exactly two, consciously."

### F17 · MINOR · 10-wiring.md:10.14, 10.27, Common mistakes
Persona: first-time
Quote: "If those are swapped nothing works and the SSR sees the wrong side of the load." / "get this backwards and the SSR either never fires or latches on."
Problem: LOAD 1/2 on an AC SSR are a series switch — swapping them changes nothing electrically (LDO's assignment is convention: photo + LED side). Reversed INPUT 3/4 means "never fires", not "latches on". Over-stated consequences dilute the one real warning (3/4 polarity, and a shorted SSR = bed permanently live, which 10.21 tests).
Fix: 10.14: "Convention from LDO's photo; keep it so the photos and 10.78 stay comparable." 10.27 ⚠: "reversed = never fires; the dangerous failure is a *shorted* relay, which 10.21 and 10.78 test."

## Chapter verdicts

- **Ch 10 — buildable by a first-timer as written: with fixes.** The mains and 24 V *connections* are well guarded (colour-out-loud, pull-tests, verbatim LDO), but the Checkpoint #1 ritual has two holes (F1 node list, F2 power-on sequence/live probing/who-is-in-the-room) and three cross-chapter contradictions (F3 Ch 09 duplication, F4 PE screw, F6 diagram 06). Weakest steps: **10.18**, **10.23**, **10.12**.
- **Ch 11 — buildable by a first-timer as written: with fixes.** The Part A/Part B boundary is impossible to miss (intro, Read-first, ⛔ header, Pause, split checkpoint) and the panel/clip/foam mapping is followable and arithmetically closed. The skirt ring is not: fan-per-side is wrong (F12), the rear run order and the in-situ belt guard are misread from p.222 (F13), and the bottom panel's door function is unstated (F14). Weakest steps: **11.11/11.15–11.17**, **11.14**, **11.21**.

## Praise
- LDO's Checkpoint 1 quoted verbatim and turned into per-row tables; "say the colour out loud" and "trace it with a finger" are exactly the right rituals for a two-person bench.
- 10.31's label table, 10.55–10.57's Rev D+ deltas and the 10.42 short-B-cable alternate route will each save a first build hours.
- Ch 11's panel-stack table with clip arithmetic that closes to zero, the "test-fit one 4 mm and one 6 mm clip" gate, and the magnet-polarity marker trick (11.36) are the best "catch it before it costs" writing in either chapter.
- Pause lines consistently say what *not* to leave half-done (mains cores, VHB, magnets, a door on one hinge).
