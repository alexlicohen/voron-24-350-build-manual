### G1 · BLOCKER · 07-ab-belts.md:07.17–07.22 (also intro ¶, 07.4, 07.23, Checkpoint 07, Common mistakes, `assets/diagrams/03-corexy-belt-path.svg` B panel + legend)
Persona: first-time
Quote: 07.17 Do: "Take the B belt end right along the X extrusion to the right XY joint and wrap it 90° round the joint's plain stack … smooth back on the bearings" (image: p.136). 07.22 Do: "Take the returning run to the left XY joint, wrap it 90° round the joint's toothed idler … teeth meshing with it" (image: p.138). Intro ¶: "each belt leaves the carriage smooth-back-on one joint's plain stack and comes back teeth-on the other joint's toothed 20T idler."
Problem: The B route is written as the reverse traversal of the manual's, and that traversal is not threadable. p.131 clamps *both* first ends in the **left** carriage half with both free lengths running **west** (the stubs sit on the left half's west face; p.139 shows the right half taking its two ends from the east). So B's first turn is the **left** XY joint, not the right — and p.105 (front elevation) fixes what it meets there: left joint = F695 pair **upper** / 20T idler **lower**; right joint = 20T **upper** / F695 **lower**. B is the lower belt (p.75 A pulley at 16.5 mm, p.79 B at 6.5 mm), so B's *first* turn is **teeth-on** the left joint's lower 20T, turning **forward** to the front-left idler, and its *last* turn is **smooth** on the right joint's lower F695. The manual's B pages are in exactly that order: p.136 left = left joint, belt from the carriage wrapping the toothed idler and heading forward; p.136 right = front-left U-turn; p.137 left = outer left run heading rearward past the left joint; p.137 right = B-drive S-wrap; p.138 left = rear-right (A drive) stack; p.138 right = right joint, belt on the **lower plain stack with A's toothed idler visible above it**. The p.127 insets agree (left joint: belt on the toothed idler; right joint: belt below the toothed idler). Consequences as written: (1) 07.17 cannot be done from the left-half clamp; (2) 07.17's picture (p.136) shows a toothed idler under text that says "plain stack, smooth back" — the exact confusion F3 was meant to remove; (3) 07.22's picture (p.138) shows B on a plain stack with a toothed idler *above* it under text that says "wrap the toothed idler" — a first-timer lifts B onto A's idler. The F3 framing "leave smooth / return teeth-on" is true for A only; the invariant that is actually fixed by the hardware is **per joint**: left joint = A smooth (upper F695) + B teeth (lower 20T); right joint = A teeth (upper 20T) + B smooth (lower F695). The tooth-contact *claims* in the chapter and the SVG (A teeth at the right joint, B teeth at the left) are correct; the direction, step order, page mapping and the "leaving/returning" wording are not. A's route (07.10–07.16, p.132→p.135) is right and threadable.
Fix: Re-order the B section to the manual's direction and re-map pages — 07.17 "B: leave the carriage **left** along X to the **left** XY joint; wrap its **lower, toothed** 20T idler **teeth-on** (Ch 05 Step 05.31), turning **forward** down the left Y — this is B's first turn and it is teeth-on by design; the plain stack above it is A's" (p.136 left); 07.18 "U-turn at the front-left idler, smooth back; come back rearward on the outer run" (p.136 right; keep the M3×40 tip here); 07.19 "run rearward up the left outer run, past the left XY joint at its own height" (p.137 left); 07.20 "S-wrap the B motor pulley at the rear-left" (p.137 right); 07.21 "right along the rear extrusion, 90° on the rear-right (A drive) idler stack, smooth back" (p.138 left); 07.22 "forward down the right Y (single run) to the **right** XY joint; wrap its **lower plain** F695 stack **smooth-back-on**, under A's toothed idler, and run left along X to the carriage; leave the tail loose for 07.24" (p.138 right). Rewrite 07.4's trace in the same direction. Intro ¶ → "…except at the XY joints, where each belt meets one toothed 20T idler and one plain F695 pair: A rides the left joint's upper plain stack and the right joint's upper toothed idler; B rides the left joint's lower toothed idler and the right joint's lower plain stack. Whether a face is teeth-on is fixed by which joint and which height — never twist a belt to change it." 07.23 Check and Checkpoint 07 → name the joints, drop "where each belt returns". Common mistakes → "the teeth-on turn at an XY joint (A at the right joint, B at the left)". SVG: reverse the B panel's arrows so B leaves the carriage leftward; swap its labels ("leaving turn: TEETH ON this joint's 20T idler" at the left joint, "return turn: smooth back on the F695 pair" at the right); legend "20T idler at the RETURN XY joint" → "20T idler — A at the right joint (Step 07.16), B at the left joint (Step 07.17)"; note that both X-runs physically lie in front of the X extrusion (the panels draw one behind it). Re-derive the per-step Source lines from the mapping above.
