### F1 · BLOCKER · 13-initial-startup.md:Tools, Step 13.22, 13.27
Persona: first-time
Quote: "a second window with `RESTART` already typed and unsent (your software E-stop)" / "Now have `RESTART` typed and unsent in the console. Send `G28 X`."
Problem: `RESTART` is not an emergency stop. Klipper only pulls `M112` out of the queue while another command is executing (`gcode.py` L461-464 scans pending lines for M112; everything else waits). A `RESTART` sent during a homing move that is driving the gantry into the frame or the nozzle past the pin does nothing until the move finishes. The first-timer has been told this is the stop and will sit there pressing Enter. `M112` appears nowhere in the manual.
Fix: Replace every "RESTART typed and unsent" with: "`M112` typed and unsent in the console — or the red Emergency Stop button in Mainsail's top bar / the touchscreen E-stop, both of which send M112. After a stop, `FIRMWARE_RESTART` brings Klipper back." Apply at Tools, 13.22 Do/Check, 13.23, 13.24–13.26 (jogs with Z unhomed), and 13.27, which must add: "Watch the descent with M112 ready. If the nozzle is not going to land on the pin shaft, stop it before it reaches the bed — the homing move continues to `position_min: -5` and will drive the nozzle into the rear extrusion." Ch 06b.2–06b.5 (not mine) inherit the same wording.

### F3 · MAJOR · 13-initial-startup.md:Step 13.36 ↔ 13.37 (and Common mistakes, Checkpoint 13)
Persona: first-time
Quote: "**You are doing this hot, and Klipper assumes cold: send one extra `TESTZ Z=-0.1` before `ACCEPT`.**" / 13.37: "`G0 X175 Y175 Z0 F1200`. Slide the paper under the nozzle. **Check:** The paper drags but is not pinned"

Rewrite the reason: "the paper test leaves the nozzle ~0.1 mm high; 13.42 closes that gap live and commits it." Fix the example (`position_endstop: -0.750`). Delete the Common-mistakes bullet "Forgetting the extra TESTZ" and the Checkpoint phrase "with the extra `TESTZ Z=-0.1`". If you want to keep the −0.1, 13.37 must test at `Z0.1`, not `Z0`.

### F4 · MAJOR · 13-initial-startup.md:Step 13.42
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Once the squish is right, make it permanent with `Z_OFFSET_APPLY_ENDSTOP` then `SAVE_CONFIG`. Keep the cube."
Problem: Read in sequence with "watch the whole first layer… live-adjust", this is done mid-print. `SAVE_CONFIG` ends in `gcode.request_restart('restart')` (`configfile.py` L403) with no print-in-progress guard — Klipper restarts and the cube print dies at layer 1–2. Fix: "Babystep during the first layer and note the total (Mainsail shows it next to the Z-offset buttons). **Let the cube finish.** Then `Z_OFFSET_APPLY_ENDSTOP` and `SAVE_CONFIG` — SAVE_CONFIG restarts Klipper and would abort a running print."

### F6 · MAJOR · 13-initial-startup.md:Step 13.40 / 14-calibration.md:Step 14.7
Persona: first-time
Quote: 14.7: "Heat the hotend to the ASA print temperature (260 °C) with filament loaded and the extruder engaged."
Problem: No step in Ch 08, 11, 13 or 14 loads filament (grep: only the spool-holder arm at 11.43). This is the first time filament enters the machine; a first-timer does not know to cut the tip, open the CW2 latch, feed to the gears, or that `G1 E…` refuses below `min_extrude_temp: 170`. The LDO config ships `LOAD_FILAMENT TEMP=<n>` (L723: moves to the front, heats, extrudes 100 mm slowly) and nobody mentions it. Also 14.7's `G1 E100 F60` is issued in absolute E mode; it only extrudes 100 mm if E happens to be 0 — add `M83` (or `G92 E0`) first.
Fix: Add "Load filament" to 13.40 (or as 14.7's first Do): spool on the holder, PTFE through the retainer, cut the tip square, `LOAD_FILAMENT TEMP=260` (or: heat, open the CW2 latch, push filament to the gears, close, `M83`, `G1 E30 F300` until it extrudes), wipe the nozzle. Put `M83` before `G1 E100 F60` in 14.7.
