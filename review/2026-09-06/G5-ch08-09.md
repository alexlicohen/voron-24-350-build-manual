### G6 · MINOR · 09-electronics-bay.md:09.30 (deck notch; shaft-out reminder)
Persona: first-time
Quote: "Reach in through the open rear of the inverted frame to the rear end of the bed extrusion… Slide the body along the extrusion — front/back, not sideways — until the pin is about 1.5 mm clear" / "lift it out for this step and drop it back in the next time the printer is upright, before Ch 13 Step 13.24 needs it"
Problem: With the bay open upward there is no "rear" to reach through — the extrusion's side face is in front of you. The one alignment constraint visible from the bay is never named: the body sits under the deck and its pin rises through the deck panel's rear notch (09.4 "notch faces the back"); slid past the notch the pin is under acrylic. The shaft-out instruction has no later hook — Checkpoint 09 says "shaft free", Ch 10 never flips the printer, and 13.24 assumes the shaft is in.
Fix: "…the extrusion's side face is right in front of you in the bay. The pin has to come up through the deck's rear notch (09.4) — that sets the position to within a few mm; fine-tune to ~1.5 mm from the plate edge (p.161 detail)." Add to Checkpoint 09: "if the 5 mm shaft is out (un-notched, 09.29), it is bagged and labelled `probe shaft — refit before 13.24`."
