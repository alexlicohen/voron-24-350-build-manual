### F2 · MAJOR · 00-before-you-start.md:"Before the kit ships" banner, 00.13–00.16, Hardware table, 00.9 — vs print/B00 Step B00.7

### F3 · MAJOR · 00-before-you-start.md:00.21 — vs 02-z-drives.md:02.06/02.08 and 05-gantry.md rail steps (05.x "Tape the carriage to the middle of the rail … slide a printed MGN9 guide onto each end")

Fix: 00.21 → "Leave both end stops on in the bag.
